# Appendix: Atwood's Table Talk and Memorabilia

## Table Talk and Memorabilia of Mary Anne Atwood (Begun August 1860)



### 1. Chapter Core Thesis
The Appendix ("Table Talk and Memorabilia") contains Mary Anne Atwood's mature reflections, dictations, and notes recorded decades after the initial 1850 publication and suppression of *A Suggestive Inquiry*. It serves as an unvarnished, direct, and explicit commentary on the practical mechanics of Hermeticism, clarifying the exact relationship between mesmerism, auto-magnetism, the three lives in man (Terrestrial, Celestial, Infernal), the nature of Will and Light, and the ultimate necessity of Christian self-sacrifice for divine palingenesia [1850–1854, 1868–1870, 1909–1911, 2003–2004].



### 2. Section-by-Section Breakdown

#### I. The Three Lives & Microcosmic Anatomy (Notes 1–11, 25–26, 88)
* **The Terrestrial Life (Animal / Head):** Sensuous, head-centered, ruling sensory cognition and animal appetite in ordinary fallen existence [1868–1869].
* **The Celestial Life (Vegetable / Heart & Solar Plexus):** Emotional, instinctive, and receptive; localized in the epigastric region (the *Archaeus* of Paracelsus) [1869].
* **The Infernal Life (Mineral / Lumbar Region & Will):** The deepest, voluntary, and mineral life-force seated in the lumbar region; it represents the raw, unrefined Will (*Saturn*) and the primary magnetic power of existence [1857–1858, 1869].
* **Reversal of the Order:** The Great Work consists of inverting the natural fallen order—withdrawing consciousness from the Terrestrial/head life, descending into the Infernal/lumbar Will, and raising the mineral fire up through the Celestial/heart life to be purified and divinized [1857–1858, 1870, 2003–2004].

#### II. Mesmerism, Auto-Magnetism, and the Cross (Notes 9–10, 30, 79, 130)
* **Vertical vs. Transverse Passes:** Perpendicular/vertical passes draw consciousness inward away from sense, while transverse passes restore outward sensory awareness. The intersection of these two motions forms the physical and metaphysical Cross ($+$) [1858–1859].
* **Auto-Magnetism / Self-Operation:** While external mesmerism uses an operator to temporarily loosen the vital spirit, authentic Hermeticism requires *auto-magnetism*—where the practitioner's own purified, rational Divine Will (*Sulfur*) acts directly upon their own vital spirit (*Mercury*) [1851, 1857, 1873, 1960].
* **The Danger of Self-Will:** If the unlocked vital power is seized by the egoic self-will, it becomes demoniacal ("Egyptian sorcery"); it is righteous only when submitted in passivity to the Divine Will [1850–1852, 1965–1966].

#### III. The Mechanics of Fermentation & Regeneration (Notes 12–23, 31–38, 138)
* **Fermentation of Life:** Fermentation is the true image of spiritual transformation. It is an ascending process where life, fermented by its kindred light, raises the subject into a higher degree of perfection without losing its essential continuity [1853, 1866–1867, 1964].
* **The Salt, Sulphur, and Mercury in the Blood:** What changes during alchemical transformation is the magnetic attraction in the arterial blood, re-ordering the three principles (Attraction/Salt, Circulation/Mercury, Repulsion/Sulphur) [1864, 1898–1900].
* **The Ashes (*Caput Mortuum* / Our Pigmy):** The physical body's underlying principle is preserved in the *Caput Mortuum* or "Ashes" (*Diadem of the Body*), from which the entire resurrected frame is reconstituted [1865].

#### IV. Philosophical & Mythological Concordances (Notes 41–50, 103, 124–126, 161)
* **Homeric & Classical Myths as Hermetic Blueprints:** The Trojan War, the Iliad, the Odyssey, the Golden Fleece, and the labors of Hercules are exact allegories of the internal psychical struggle and the insphering of the lower life [1880–1882, 1982–1983].
* **Behmen, Neoplatonists, and Oken:** Jacob Boehme is praised as the deepest writer on the internal life-center, transcending the Neoplatonists by speaking from direct, conscious *in-seeing* (*experience*) rather than mere scientific theory [1887, 1914–1915]. Lorenz Oken is highlighted for evolving Causal Truth into outward natural forms [1868].



### 3. Primary Conceptual Takeaways
* **The Microcosmic Trinity:** Man possesses three distinct modes of life/consciousness (Terrestrial/Animal/Head, Celestial/Vegetable/Heart, Infernal/Mineral/Lumbar).
* **The True Philosopher's Stone:** The perfected, regenerated human soul is the actual Philosopher's Stone in its metalline/mineral perfection [1853, 1879, 2002].
* **Auto-Magnetism as the Key:** True alchemy transcends external mesmerism through self-directed, internal magnetic fermentation driven by the Divine Will [1857, 1873, 1960].
* **The Universal Law of the Cross:** Reversing the outward linear flow into a circular, eternal motion forms the metaphysical Cross, locking the Divine Light into the physical vessel [1857–1859, 1880].

### 5. Key Direct Quotes
1. *"Alchemy is an universal art of vital chemistry, which by fermenting the human spirit, purifies, and, finally dissolving it, opens the elementary germ into new life and consciousness; and the Philosopher’s Stone is the efflux of such a life, drawn to a focus and made manifest as a concrete Essence of Light..."* (Note 5 [1853–1854])
2. *"The perpendicular line is the Divine line in all things; the transverse line is the contrary; the two united form a cross +. In mesmerizing and de-mesmerizing we form a +. It is curious that by this peculiar action the perpendicular line should withdraw you from sense while the transverse restore you to it."* (Note 9 [1858–1859])
3. *"The soul in its perfection is the Christian philosopher’s stone. The soul in its metalline perfection is the philosopher’s stone."* (Notes 2002 [2002])
