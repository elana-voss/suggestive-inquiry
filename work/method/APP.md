# Method material — APP Table Talk and Memorabilia of Mary Anne Atwood (Appendix, begun August 1860)

The Appendix is a later collection of numbered aphorisms, items (1)–(181) plus unnumbered closing notes, not a continuous argument. Nearly all of it is in Atwood's own voice. Where she cites an authority, the voice tag says so. Paragraph refs are `APP ¶n`.

## The subject (what the work is done on)

- **The lowest, "third" or "mineral" life in man.** The Art perfects this life, and the Stone is the same life brought to completion. Fallen man lives in it now.
  - "The Hermetic Art is a perfectioning of the third or lowest or the mineral life in man" (APP ¶44)
  - Also: "the Stone of the Wise is the perfected lowest life, its true consummation, which is mineral in its origin" (APP ¶46); "Man is fallen; he is in the third or lowest life" (APP ¶35); "the release or setting free the third or lowest life" (APP ¶2); "The third life, the mineral or lowest life in man, brings you into contact with the Macrocosmic mineral life" (APP ¶59)
  - stated; ATWOOD. See Open questions on "third life".

- **One's own life as it now is: "take that stone".** The stone to be taken is one's present life, hardened like stone, and it is taken in order to be fermented.
  - "they say "take that stone", meaning take life as it is and ferment it" (APP ¶132)
  - Also: "We are all compacted in a certain petrification, i.e., life in each is as a stone in this first being" (APP ¶132)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **The human vital spirit in its natural, impure state.** It is always unformed and coming-to-be, and it needs rebirth.
  - "The body of brass is the impure natural-born vital spirit." (APP ¶52)
  - Also: "the chaos of te Vital Spirit (which always is in chaos, i.e., always coming to be, never is, and whilst in this life wants the rebirth)" (APP ¶165); "by fermenting the human spirit" (APP ¶6); "the art of fermenting the human vital spirit" (APP ¶68)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **The seat of the work: the body, the blood and the limbs.** The process runs in the blood. The members of the body are the spirit's laboratory.
  - "The process takes place in and through the human body in the blood" (APP ¶6)
  - Also: "The life acts through the sensuous and sensible spirit in the blood" (APP ¶91); "they are a laboratory that is of and for the spirit to work in" (APP ¶16); "the principle of sense or the sensuous medium in the blood" (APP ¶109)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **The impure vital atmosphere around each person.** All of a person's thoughts and desires are imaged in it. Clairvoyants can see it, and its density shows as a person's darkness or brightness. The whole Mystery aims to purify it.
  - "The whole process of the Mystery was intended to purify the impure vital atmosphere which surrounds each individual life." (APP ¶73)
  - Also: "Its density or lightness is the cause of the darkness or brightness which may be seen in men." (APP ¶73)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **The soul's "vaporous vehicle".** This is the material principle taken on at conception. It hides the soul's original paternal light so as to bring it out into sense.
  - "The vaporous vehicle is the material principle in the conception, it obscures the pure paternal light of life --- the generative light, in order to bring it out into sense." (APP ¶121)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **A hidden light or gold-seed inside that life.** The ferment that starts the work lies concealed in ordinary life. It goes by several names: Divine Light, Essence of Gold, Sulphur, the First, the masculine or divine ferment.
  - "the rational ferment, the pure aurific seed of Light, is hidden in this life of ours" (APP ¶35)
  - Also: "The Divine Light, the Essence of Gold, is hidden in the self-begotten life." (APP ¶70); "The Sulphur, the pure light of life, is bound down in the inferior mineral region of life" (APP ¶125); "the Third Principle, i.e., the sidereal and elementary birth, to use Boehme's language, is the medium of perceiving the First, which is hidden in its mineral life" (APP ¶27); "the masculine ferment, the sulphur or divine ferment (which is doubly hidden and beyond in this life of ours)" (APP ¶182); "the central hidden light (there's the mystery) is hidden in the voluntary contrariation" (APP ¶125)
  - stated; ATWOOD. The Boehme terms are used with endorsement.

- **Salt: the will-substance, bound in the lowest region.** It must be drawn up into the medial life.
  - "The Slat is the voluntary substance, and is bound up in the lowest region, the generative mineral life" (APP ¶77)
  - Also: "There is no true body but Will; it is the principle of the body --- the Salt." (APP ¶65); "The Salt, the will" (APP ¶182)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **"The fire" is one's own will, chaotic and still potential.** It is the ground on which the inner re-creation is built.
  - "the fire here is our chaotic will in potentiality; it is the basis of our microcosmic recreation" (APP ¶97)
  - stated; ATWOOD, glossing Kirchberger's "fire"

- **The Philosophic Matter as light drawn out of life through suffering.**
  - "The Philosophic Matter is an excruciated ens of Light; alight extracted through anguish from Life." (APP ¶84)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **The Philosophical matter as the body's entelechy carrying the spirit.** The text does not gloss "entelechy". The soul is called the body's ultimate perfection and a mesothesis between two spheres.
  - "The Philosophical matter is the entelechy of body bearing the spirit, the antecedent cause, right up through." (APP ¶178)
  - Also: "The human soul is more than a mere resultant of body; it is its ultimate perfection, for it is a mesothesis between two spheres." (APP ¶178)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **The One Thing is the psychical essence, identified with the nervous system.**
  - "The One Thing; it is our psychical essence; our nervous system." (APP ¶176)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **The First Matter is reached by going back in consciousness, not by searching outwardly.** It lies just before the first act of consciousness. Atwood cites Plotinus's account of how it is generated but calls that account "merely the metaphysic of it".
  - "The First Matter is not to be found on earth, as the philosophers say; you must go back into the original of life from which it springs; go back in consciousness. It is immediately antecedent to the first consciousness." (APP ¶137)
  - Also: "The pure matter of the philosophers is not to be found on earth" (APP ¶43; QUOTED: Raymond Lully, endorsed); "a recessure of reason behind itself into the indefinite intermediate between reason and the Absolute" (APP ¶137; QUOTED: Plotinus, qualified)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Proteus: undivided universal life before it becomes individual.** All descriptions of the Matter refer to it. Its dissolved essence is the philosophers' mercury. It is hard to find, and its last disguise is a tree, which seems to be the tree of the Golden Bough.
  - "the one universal entity of life, the omnia in omnibus before manifestation and individuality, the thing of which you may assert everything; Proteus." (APP ¶138)
  - Also: "its dissolved essence which is the mercury of the Philosophers, Proteus" (APP ¶144); "The Protean life is very difficult of discovery" (APP ¶171); "This tree appears to be identical with that bearing the Golden Bough" (APP ¶171)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **The Universal Ether.** Each person holds a portion of it, enclosed at conception. The alchemists' "four elements" are the four principles of this Ether, which is their quintessence.
  - "That ether is the omnia in omnibus of the Hermetic Riddle. What we have is a scrap of that ether shut in at our birth-conception." (APP ¶119)
  - Also: "When speaking of the four elements, Alchemists refer to the four elements of the Universal Ether." (APP ¶69); "It is the Quintessence of both worlds." (APP ¶69); "The Ether is the universal extended Matter; the Divine posit everywhere; life." (APP ¶179)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **The "magical earth" lies at one's own center.** Atwood glosses Eirenaeus's gold thrown into "bottomless" Mercury as an inward search. What is found is a pure earth of the air or vegetable life that begins the Regeneration. The mineral earth belongs to the lower life.
  - "in finding the magical earth in yourself, in your own center, you find that which is in the air or the vegetable life, that pure earth which commences the Regeneration, the medial life" (APP ¶89)
  - Also: ""Take that matter which is gold", says Eirenaeus, "and throw it into Mercury, such a Mercury as is bottomless, whose center it can never find but by discovering its own"" (APP ¶89; QUOTED: Eirenaeus, glossed by Atwood); "The mineral earth belongs to the lower life and to the center of the globe" (APP ¶89)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **The universal matter can be reached at any of the three bodily centers** (head, heart, lumbar region).
  - "The universal matter is obtainable at the focus of either center." (APP ¶95)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **The Hermetic sepulcher.** It is what carries the dead Divine Life within us, and it is also that vehicle's own death.
  - "The Hermetic sepulcher is the vehicle of the dead Divine Life within us, and at the same time it is the death of itself --- 'idem est sepulchrum ac cadaver'." (APP ¶19)
  - stated; ATWOOD

## The agent (what does the work: fire, spirit, light, intellect…)

- **The Universal Spirit or Vital Spirit, which the individual draws on.** The Vital Spirit exists both in the universe and in the individual. In the Art the individual draws on the universal, and in one of its forms it is the principle of all growth.
  - "In the Art the individual draws the Universal." (APP ¶29)
  - Also: "The Vital Spirit is in the Universal as well as in the individual." (APP ¶28); "The Universal Spirit is in one form the Principle of all growth" (APP ¶4); "a development of the art by which a particular spirit (to use Vaughan's words) is allied to the universal" (APP ¶53)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Another life of the same kind, as ferment.** Life is fermented by a like life. The fire of one life enters the same fire in another, opens it, and sets free the celestial Life and Light. Both lives are dissolved and a third is produced.
  - "The fire of the natural life entering into and fermenting the natural fire --- the same life in another --- opens the last, and develops and excites and sets free the celestial Life and Light --- that is one main principle." (APP ¶38)
  - Also: "ferment it, that is by another life, which will dissolve and kill it as well as itself; both will be reproduced in a third" (APP ¶132); "this life being fermented by this life, its similar, leads into the common trance" (APP ¶3)
  - stated; ATWOOD. Whether "another life" means another person is open (see Open questions).

- **Will: the only substance and the universal lodestone.** All things are held in Will. What Will has bound only Will can unbind, and Saturn is a name for Will.
  - "for Will has substance, nay, is the only substance; it is the Universal Loadstone of Existence" (APP ¶8)
  - Also: "Everything is in Will. All in the beginning is Will" (APP ¶86); "All is held in potentiality in Will." (APP ¶81); "a Saturn (i.e., Will) has the Key to the prison where Sulphur lies bound; for that which Will has bound, Will only can unbind" (APP ¶125); "The law of Will in these higher states of being is to have motion from its own center." (APP ¶8)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **The Universal Will (Wisdom, the "great Salt Sea") acting on the self-will.** The Universal Will is the agent and the self-will the patient. It dissolves the self-will's false forms (desires and phantasms) by first destroying the faculties and then reproducing them transmuted.
  - "The great Salt Sea (Wisdom, the Universal Will) dissipates the impure sulphurs (desires, phantasms) in the individual will as the strokes of the sun's rays dissipate the darkness of night." (APP ¶94)
  - Also: "The Universal Will being represented as agent dissolves the false forms in the self-will represented as patient." (APP ¶94); "the effort of the Universal Will is to draw them into its service by first destroying them, and then reproducing them in a transmuted form" (APP ¶92)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Love: the pull from above.** The feminine principle (Wisdom, the heart life) draws the self-willed source upward like a magnet. Only love can move it from self-consciousness into self-oblivion.
  - "it is love which attracts. Nothing else could move that principle from its self-conscious center into self-oblivion." (APP ¶98)
  - Also: "The strength of the attraction of that which is above (the feminine principle, --- Wisdom, --- the heart life), forces, draws, attracts as by a magnet, the self-willed source upwards" (APP ¶98)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Emotion and conscience as ferment.** Emotion is the midpoint by which mind moves matter. Conscience striking the self-life ferments it and brings out a third, new life. Contrition does the same work, and heart-emotions give the Adepts' powers their quality.
  - "It is by emotion that mind is moved into matter. Emotion is a mid-point between the two; conscience strikes upon the self-life, and, when it does so, effectually ferments it and evolves from it a third form of life, a new birth of life." (APP ¶80)
  - Also: "The powers developed, of which the Adepts speak, are produced from emotional sources in the heart" (APP ¶37); "contrition, live, purity, mercy, humility, are such emotions" (APP ¶37); "Emotion beings forth essence" (APP ¶37)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Light, especially the Rational Light.** Alchemy ferments the vital spirit "in and by its own Light". The Rational Light (also "honey of the bees" and "aurific seed") is the strongest light. Hermetism is the magnetism of Light under its own law, and that law is Wisdom.
  - "Alchemy is a process of fermenting the vital Spirit in and by its own Light." (APP ¶27)
  - Also: "The Rational Light is the strongest of all lights, and overcomes every other." (APP ¶168); "Hermetism is the magnetism of Light according to its own law, which Law is its emanating spirit called wisdom, for it is the Wisdom." (APP ¶47); "The entrance of the essential light of the reason into the chaos of te Vital Spirit" (APP ¶165)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **The law of Light, moving through Love.** The will-moved mind must serve it, or it breaks and becomes demoniacal.
  - "there is a law of Light, which must move through Love in the will, and so act on the will-moved mind, and to which it must be subservient, else it breaks and becomes demoniacal" (APP ¶9)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Sulphur, the masculine or divine ferment, is what perfects the matter.** The pure matter obtained first is passive and cannot perfect itself. The active, doubly hidden ferment has to be set free to complete it.
  - "you must set free the masculine ferment, the sulphur or divine ferment (which is doubly hidden and beyond in this life of ours) to effect the perfection of the matter" (APP ¶182)
  - Also: "All you get first of all is a pure matter which is passive, having no power of perfecting itself; it is the attractive power." (APP ¶182)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Mind as the mover, Vulcan as its instrument.** Vulcan (Faber) is Motion. Vulcan stirs up the philosophers' fire but is not that fire, and is lame because motion in this life runs in a line and never returns into its own principle. A spirit-man inside the natural man moves the Ether.
  - "The Vulcan or Faber is the instrument of the motive agent --- the mind." (APP ¶130)
  - Also: "The Vulcan of the Alchemists is Motion." (APP ¶129); "Vulcan is not the fire, the ignis Philosophorum, but the stirrer up of the fire." (APP ¶129); "There is a spirit-man within the natural man and his work is to move the Ether." (APP ¶139)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **The spirit as its own teacher and worker.** The spirit teaches its art and the artist advances by obeying it. Once freed, it works by its own law.
  - "The spirit teaches its own art, and according as it is obeyed the artist goes on developing the way for him to advance itself to perfection." (APP ¶40)
  - Also: "The spirit freed works by itself, by its own law, ignis et Azoth tibi sufficient; the wonder must be to see it working its miracle." (APP ¶131)
  - stated; ATWOOD. "ignis et Azoth" is unglossed (see Open questions).

- **Divine Will through voice and utterance.** The Divine Will uses the human voice to conduct Light through the fire. Mind's speech into the pure Ether gives things their specific form. If The Name speaks in a person, it works its own change.
  - "the Divine Will makes use of the human voice as an organ for conducting the Light, through the fire" (APP ¶97; QUOTED: Kirchberger, endorsed: "It strikes me that he is right in this")
  - Also: "The speech of Mind uttered into the pure Ether qualitates and specificates." (APP ¶64); "If it should speak in us it will work its own change in us" (APP ¶88)
  - stated; ATWOOD, with the Kirchberger line endorsed

- **Desire is formative.** Will by itself makes only an image. Desire for that image, united with it, makes a being. Desire gathers matter around the image of what it desires.
  - "Desire constricts the essence of what it desires and so draws matter about the image of its object." (APP ¶111)
  - Also: "Will creates an image, not a being. The will passing into desire for the image creates it by union with itself into a being" (APP ¶36)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Music and sound.** Music carries the spirit back to its origins and gives shape in the ether. The walls of Troy, enclosing the lower life, were built by Apollo's music.
  - "By music the human spirit is passed back into its original sources; the sound makes a concave in the recipient life; it is formative in the ether" (APP ¶128)
  - Also: "Those walls were said to be built by the music of Apollo. Apollo is that life" (APP ¶49)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **The invisible hand and the Ether's "hands"** (heading "Help Manual"). The bodily hand clothes an invisible hand, which works effects according to the perfection it has reached. The Ether's hands are its transverse poles, and in man they are the natural fire of the Ether in the life organism.
  - "The hands of the Ether are its transverse poles, which are formed by the divine differentiation everywhere, and are in man a natural fire ---i.e., the fire of the Ether in us, or of our life organism." (APP ¶177)
  - Also: "The hand outward is an image of the invisible hand, its clothing" (APP ¶177)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **The "orient animal": the opposite will clothed in Light**, the third rising in opposition to the first.
  - "The 'orient animal' means the animal (that is, the opposite) will which is clothed in Light; the third which rises in opposition to the first." (APP ¶21)
  - stated; ATWOOD. Which "third" and "first" are meant is open.

- **Nature's spirit works by imagination.** It wraps itself in subtle ("aromal") emanations to realize what it imagines. The same constructive power works in us when "the constructing organ is called into play".
  - "The spirit of nature is all imagination, ideality, fancy. She wraps herself about with what you call aromal essences, aromal emanations, in order to realize her imaginings." (APP ¶122)
  - Also: "She enjoys reconstructing in the world, just as she does in us when the constructing organ is called into play." (APP ¶122)
  - stated; ATWOOD. Its link to the work is only that one clause.

## The operator (requirements: knowledge, moral state, will, guidance, secrecy…)

- **A pure will and intention.** Everything depends on intention, and the hard part is finding it. Before consciously entering the lowest life, which is full of defilement and temptation, the operator's will and purpose must be wholly pure.
  - "it is therefore necessary, before going into it consciously, that the will and purpose be perfectly pure and simple in this return of the reason to its First Cause" (APP ¶27)
  - Also: "all depends on the willing and the intention" (APP ¶35); "The difficulty is to find this principium, the intention" (APP ¶35); "the motive is segregated from the whole life as a pure Light allied to that which it seeks" (APP ¶27)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **The will must serve God's law.** Power gained is legitimate only on this condition. Taken into self-will it becomes diabolical, which marks the difference between Moses and the Egyptian magicians.
  - "Power thus gained is only legitimate, just and righteous when the will subserves the commandment and law of God; taken into the self-will and used accordingly it becomes diabolical" (APP ¶4)
  - Also: "Magicians, like the Egyptians of that day, drew only from their own strength" (APP ¶5)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **The will surrendered passively to the Divine Will.** Even beginning takes entire self-devotion. All true religion is preparation of the will for this.
  - "regenerated or born again as an offspring of his own will, given up in passivity to the Divine Will" (APP ¶79)
  - Also: "nothing but entire self-devotion is of any use even to begin with" (APP ¶90); "All true religion in this life is a preparation of the Will for this final work." (APP ¶87)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Aim first at the Universal, single-mindedly.** Seek the Kingdom of God first, not a finite end. Finite ends sought first have to be given up at every stage.
  - ""Seek ye first the Kingdom of God and His righteousness and all these things shall be added unto you"" (APP ¶134)
  - Also: "is really the shortest as well as the truest road to the attainment of all that is promised or can be looked for" (APP ¶134)
  - Further: "the will should aim singly at the highest good lest it create for itself idols" (APP ¶133); "If you seek a finite end first, i.e., before you seek the Universal --- the Kingdom of God --- which you are ordered to do, you will have to give up the former at every stage" (APP ¶135)
  - stated; ATWOOD, applying Scripture

- **Act from the medial life (faith), not the head life.** Do not reach for the highest or creative life. The regenerate give up the animal-rational head life, though not "the Divine inseeing of the Celestial life".
  - "We have no right to be efficient in the present head life; our business is to be efficient in the medial life --- in faith; then we are efficient for the life immediately over us --- the divine." (APP ¶45)
  - Also: "Man must not dare to aspire to that highest life which is over this birth and which is typified by the terrestrial or head life, since if he were there he would become Creative --- he would be taking to himself the Divine Will." (APP ¶35); "Those who are Hermetically regenerated in this world have given up the Head life or the animal, the rational life" (APP ¶60); "they enter into our Lord's life by a sacrifice in faith" (APP ¶60)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Humility, and reason informed by love.** The Universal Reason answers only the humble seeker. What one finds depends on the faculty one seeks with: selfish desire reaches only the lower atmosphere, while the Divine Light region needs reason informed by love and purified by humiliation.
  - "What you find all depends on what you look with; ontologically you find what you seek, that is, the ontological quality of the faculty you seek with determines what you find" (APP ¶170)
  - Also: "To the true seeker after truth in humility, not in pride, it responds." (APP ¶116); "the proper habituation of the high Reason when informed by the Love, and after it has been purified by passing through the humiliation" (APP ¶170); "if you seek with a selfish, covetous, desiring spirit you find its object in the lower spiritual atmosphere" (APP ¶170); "We are all of us drawn to our Ideal as to a star" (APP ¶169)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Patience and measure.** No haste, and no excess, not even in the effort to be good. The will follows God's timing, and going beyond one's given capacity lets the contrary principle react.
  - "it is the duty of man's will to follow God's Will as He, according to His time and measure, opens the way" (APP ¶110)
  - Also: "Festina lente, Ne quid nimis, are sayings very applicable to the work" (APP ¶110); "too much haste to be good or perfect involves a self-willed action which defeats the end" (APP ¶110); "if we do, it gives power to the contrary principle to react upon the over-run" (APP ¶110)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Lived experience, not theory.** The universal must be developed in a person before it can be understood. Alchemy is understood fully only by those actually working the process. Many alchemists saw only in theory.
  - "you must have the Universal developed in you before you can understand the Universal" (APP ¶65)
  - Also: "Alchemy can be fully understood only by those who have entered on and are working the philosophic process" (APP ¶181); "Many of the Alchemists only saw theoretically" (APP ¶76); "The adept regards nature inwardly and as a whole; he feels all in himself." (APP ¶180)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Two kinds of seeker: passive-loving and intellectual.** The passive, loving seeker is worked on through every surface of the spirit, slowly but surely. The intellectual seeker goes deeper fast but has more to overcome.
  - "Seekers, who are in the Divine order of Love, who are passive, are worked at every surface of the Spirit, and this takes time; for doing is more tedious than seeing, but then it is surer, it takes up everything under God's Will." (APP ¶115)
  - Also: "Intellectual seekers get quickly deeper, but have more to overcome." (APP ¶115)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Temperament.** Mars/Venus natures know by experience, close to the "vortex of life". Mercury/Jupiter natures perceive and express more clearly, but cannot feel or realize without the voluntary dominance of Saturn and Luna.
  - "their knowledge is by experience, not perception or intuition, which are the property of Mercury and Jupiter" (APP ¶113)
  - Also: "cannot feel as well, or realize without the voluntary dominance of Saturn and Luna" (APP ¶113)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Guidance comes from spirit, not from human initiation.** The descent is led by spiritual guides. Whoever imparts the spirit is moved by an influence outside himself. Initiates moved one another on by words of power, and those words, like the true tools, are found by seeking within.
  - "You are always taken down there by spiritual guides; it is never human initiation, that" (APP ¶126)
  - Also: "He who imparts the spirit to another is as the wind that blows, moved by an influence external to itself." (APP ¶41); "The Initiated moved one another on by words of power." (APP ¶100); "which are only to be found by seeking them in the subjective, the fundamental life" (APP ¶100); "The true tools also may be found on the way in; they will be given one after another as they are wanted" (APP ¶100)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Reverence and restraint in speech.** The operator must not speak The Name. The matter is too sacred for ordinary language, and merely thinking into these spheres moves them.
  - "We have no business whatsoever to speak The Name." (APP ¶88)
  - Also: "The real mystery is so very wonderful, great and close, that one cannot venture to speak of it in ordinary language." (APP ¶58); "I don't think you can think into such spheres of life without moving them, or being moved by them" (APP ¶85)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Renunciation of what is offered.** First-fruits must be sacrificed for what they promise. When all the world's riches are set before the seeker, he gives them up.
  - "they must be sacrificed for the sake of the promise they contain, would he fulfill the Divine purpose and not his own" (APP ¶74)
  - Also: "man at that moment craving the riches of the whole world set before him, gives them up" (APP ¶158)
  - stated; ATWOOD

## The procedure and its stages (in the order the chapter gives, if it gives one)

The Appendix gives no single overall order. It gives (A) summary definitions of the whole process, (B) several partial sequences, each stated in the text, and (C) separate operations, listed here in order of first appearance.

### A. The process as a whole

- **Ferment, purify, dissolve, regenerate.** The vital spirit is fermented, which purifies it. It is then dissolved, and its principle is rebuilt in a regeneration under a reversed law.
  - "Alchemy is the art of fermenting the human vital spirit in order to purify it and finally to dissolve it, so that the principle be reconstructed through a regeneration." (APP ¶68)
  - Also: "Alchemy is an universal art of vital chemistry, which by fermenting the human spirit, purifies, and, finally dissolving it, opens the elementary germ into new life and consciousness" (APP ¶6); "an artificial dissolution of the natural bond of life, resulting in a regeneration of the dissolved separated elements, under a reversed law" (APP ¶7; QUOTED: "the Hermeticists", endorsed)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Reverse the natural order of the three lives.** The lowest life is raised and born through the medial (celestial) life until it can receive the Divine. The work translates consciousness from sense back and up into its source.
  - "The aim in the Work is to reverse the whole action of these lives in their natural order." (APP ¶35)
  - Also: "this third life has to be raised up and born through the medial or celestial life, and becomes receptive of the Divine" (APP ¶35); "The whole of this mystery is a translation of the consciousness" (APP ¶83); "it may be, it should be, thrown backwards, nay upwards, into its First Source" (APP ¶83)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **The work is repeated many times, gradual but always progressing.** Each repetition is a further surrender of the will, because the full birth could not be borne at once. Each fermented spirit moves beyond its origin, and the confused order of the Hermetic texts comes from describing different repetitions.
  - "He fermentative process has to be gone over and over again" (APP ¶17)
  - Also: "The process goes over and over again, for we could not bear the birth in our own strength; it is a successive giving up of itself to be bettered on the part of Will." (APP ¶84); "The work is gradual, yet always progressive; each fermented spirit advances upon its origin." (APP ¶111); "as the Artist begins, ends, and repeats the mysterious experiment" (APP ¶17); "This Divine Wisdom in us has to be born over Andover again" (APP ¶90)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **An ascent through spheres of being, with no standing still.** At each sphere the will must follow its law of progress to the end, the Divine manifestation.
  - "The process is a passage of life through various spheres of being, in any of which the will may rest, but every one of which presents a law of progress at its development which should be pursued to the end --- the Divine manifestation" (APP ¶160)
  - Also: "There is no standing still in those spheres of perpetual motion. Upwards or downwards the will must go." (APP ¶160)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **An inquiry into life.** The Art questions the spirit in man about the first cause. Each question is dissolved into the enquirer's spirit and changes him.
  - "The Hermetic Process is a regular inquisition into life. There is motion present with interrogation." (APP ¶138)
  - Also: "The Art is a question put to Nature respecting the first cause" (APP ¶48); "To the stronghold of the Sphinx herself in the spirit in man." (APP ¶48); "in the Hermetic process there is an inquisition into life, in which every question is solved, thrown into solution" (APP ¶115); "it is light dissolving darkness until there is no more darkness or shadow of a doubt" (APP ¶115)
  - stated; ATWOOD

### B. Partial sequences the text gives

- **B1. Two fermentations "by its similar".** Ordinary life is fermented and passes into common trance (the sleep-walkers' or clairvoyant life). That life, fermented again, passes into the third life, which moves with the creative Essence.
  - "this present life may be led through the clairvoyant life into it (that is the wonderful part of it) by the fermentation of itself" (APP ¶2)
  - Also: "As this life being fermented by this life, its similar, leads into the common trance, or is entranced, so that consequent life being fermented subsequently by its similar, leads into that third life which moves in and with the creative Essence" (APP ¶3)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **B2. Three lives, two dissolutions.** Dissolving the sensuous medium opens the medial (intuitional) life. Dissolving that opens the divine life, which rectifies the whole person. "Solution" means separating off the sensuous medium.
  - "Dissolve the sensuous medium and you have the second or medial life. Dissolve the medial and you reach to the divine, which rectified the whole individual." (APP ¶182)
  - Also: "Solution refers to the separation of the sensuous medium itself. You cut off the sensuous life and get into the intuitional" (APP ¶176); "Our life is threefold. The Salt, the will, opens it secondfold." (APP ¶182)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **B3. Walls of Troy: enclosing, then solution.** The lower life is first enclosed in a sphere, "in a great resonance". Throwing down those walls is the alchemical solution, and it comes with noise.
  - "The Walls of Troy are a figure (and were meant by the poets and traditionalists to be so) of the insphering of the lower life; it is done in a great resonance." (APP ¶49)
  - Also: "The throwing down of the Walls of this is harmony-ensphered life is the Alchemical solution" (APP ¶50)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **B4. Motion, then stillness, then stirring again.** The first step is to set the spirit in motion. At one point the process falls into an indifferent stillness, and the Artist must stir the motion up again.
  - "The first step in this Philosophy is to create motion in the spirit." (APP ¶130)
  - Also: "there is a point of passivity, where there is no attraction but rather an indifference to both the life and the death; then the Artist must look to his Work, and seek to stir up the Motion" (APP ¶82; QUOTED: "The Alchemists", endorsed)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **B5. Reason's light in the chaos: images, dispersal, then union.** When the light of reason enters the Vital Spirit, it first shapes the chaos into vivid images. Sustained direct scrutiny then dissolves them. The light then seeks the Paternal (fundamental) Life, finds it through maternal instinct ("the doves of Venus"), and is joined with it. Each degree of union strengthens it for further struggle.
  - "that entrance constriucts the chaos at first into vivid images, but persisting in its direct look or intellectual perscrutination of the life, dissolves and disperses the images of which the chaos is full" (APP ¶165)
  - Also: "Then thelight goes on seeking the Paternal Life, the fundamental life; and finding it through the maternal instinct (the doves of Venus) is conjoined, and so far as it is conjoined is strengthened for further warfare." (APP ¶167)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **B6. Through Tartarus by the Golden Bough.** To reach the Paternal ens, one must pass through "the death of this life" (Tartarus), and this requires propitiation by offering the Golden Bough. Elsewhere the Golden Bough is identified with the Light that springs from reason's alliance with the higher Cause.
  - "Without the Golden Bough Aeneas could not proceed over or pass by Tartarus to see his Father. Tartarus is the death of this life." (APP ¶168)
  - Also: "By propitiation, through the offering of the Golden Bough, he is passed safely through to the Elysian regions, where the Paternal ens resides in liberty." (APP ¶168); "The Light which springs up in that alliance is the Divine aurific seed and true form of gold. That was the Golden Bough" (APP ¶152)
  - stated identities. Reading the Bough as a required propitiatory offering at this stage is interpreted (basis: ¶168 "By propitiation", ¶171 "his talisman"); ATWOOD

- **B7. Spirit, soul and body in the dissolution.** Soul and spirit separate from a breathless body but stay tied to it "as by a thread" until refixation. The freed spirit then perfects its own vehicle, the soul. The sensual medium is reduced to its last essence, and the residue gives body to the regenerate soul.
  - "In the Philosophical dissolution of the body, soul and spirit are separate; the body lies without any breath; the other two are united to it as by a thread, and this continues until the refixation takes place." (APP ¶96)
  - Also: "the spirit becoming freed from the body, carries on the perfection and purification of her own vehicle --- the soul" (APP ¶10); "The sensual medium must in the work be brought to its pivot, to its last ens. The residuum from this medium supplies body to the regenerate soul; after that the soul has passed under its Cross." (APP ¶110); "The principle of body is preserved in what they call the ashes or Caput Mortuum, and that one principle being saved the whole life is restored from it" (APP ¶26)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **B8. Passive matter, then active ferment, then return on the matrix.** The first product is a passive, attractive pure matter. The hidden masculine ferment (sulphur) must be freed to perfect it. To produce effect, the sulphur must turn back on its matrix, and each principle (active, passive, resultant) must be evolved separately.
  - "For production of effect there must be a return of the sulphur (this third, this obscure, this radical life) on its matrix. Each principle of the life --- active, passive, and resultant --- must be produced or evolved separately" (APP ¶183)
  - Also: "All you get first of all is a pure matter which is passive" (APP ¶182)
  - stated; ATWOOD

### C. Operations (in order of first appearance)

- **Reversing circulation in the blood.** The relations among the blood's principles change and its circulatory order reverses. The sensible medium goes hidden, the inner source of vitality wakes, and consciousness is drawn to the center. What actually changes is the magnetic attraction: life is drawn inward instead of outward. Repeated contritions bring this about.
  - "reversing the circulatory order, so that, the sensible medium becoming occult, the inner source of its vitality is awakened, and the consciousness at the same time being drawn centrally" (APP ¶6)
  - Also: "What is really changed is the magnetic attraction; the medial spirit is changed; life is attracted in, instead of out." (APP ¶24); "the change is wrought by reiterative contritions absorbing the sensuous medium and carrying back the purer conscious ens of light to its first life in God" (APP ¶78)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Will-power in the lumbar region "sent back".** The text does not explain what "sent back" means.
  - "There is a time when the power, the vital or willing-power, is seated in the lumbar region and is sent back." (APP ¶11)
  - stated as a stage. Its meaning (a reversal upward) is interpreted (basis: ¶33 voluntary life in the lumbar region; ¶35 reversal); ATWOOD

- **The mind relates to the macrocosmic spirit and is taught by it.** Atwood offers this tentatively.
  - "I think there is a time, when the mind comes into relation with the macrocosmic spirit, and discerns it, and is taught by it" (APP ¶12)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Mesmeric action as a cross; vertical solves, horizontal fixes.** Mesmerism traces a cross. The perpendicular (Divine) line withdraws a person from sense and the transverse line restores sense. Mesmerism is an external imitation of the Divine Art of separation and loosens spirit from body only for the moment. Perpendicular magnetism dissolves the sensuous vehicle and frees the soul; horizontal magnetism fixes.
  - "The solution of the sensuous spirit or vehicle is effected by the Perpendicular Magnetism, the fixation by the Horizontal; altitude and latitude." (APP ¶173)
  - Also: "In mesmerizing and de-mesmerizing we form a +." (APP ¶13); "the perpendicular line should withdraw you from sense while the transverse restore you to it" (APP ¶13); "The action of mesmerism is a very external way --- an imitation of the Divine Art of the separation of which Paracelsus speaks." (APP ¶73); "If you magnetize a person, he is elevated into a purer atmosphere instantly" (APP ¶91); "By the former the soul is set free --- it translates the consciousness into another medium." (APP ¶173)
  - stated; ATWOOD. What these magnetisms are in practice is not explained.

- **Satiating Saturn (the self-willed life) with Rational Light.** The self-willed life cannot be overcome by force, only by being fed from outside with Light until it bursts its husk and is transmuted.
  - "Saturn or the self-willed life can only be overcome in the process by satiation; he must be given from without plenty "of the honey of the bees", the aurific seed, the Rational Light." (APP ¶72)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Raising the Salt or Sulphur into the medial life.** The will-substance (Salt) or the light of life (Sulphur) is drawn up from the lowest region and grafted into the middle region, where it becomes light and grows.
  - "must be translated or drawn up into the medial life" (APP ¶77)
  - Also: "the Salt thus transplanted becomes Light and grows" (APP ¶77); "has to be drawn forth and transplanted into the medial region before it can grow and become manifest. It is brought up like a graft" (APP ¶125); "(Troy in the old analogy is that citadel where Apollo keeps his cattle, Mercury steals them and afterwards sacrifices them)" (APP ¶77)
  - stated; ATWOOD. Mapping the Troy cattle onto this is interpreted.

- **Purging the false forms ("sulphurs") and dying to everything, finally to one's own identity.** The forms of worldly life (desires, phantasms) must be purified or removed. The spirit must die to everything, last of all to its identity, to become a pure receptacle for the Divine Seed. Wisdom's end is called "annihilation".
  - "The human spirit has to die to everything that it now lives to, and lastly to its own identity, in order to become a simple receptacle and magnet for the Divine Seed." (APP ¶87)
  - Also: "All the false sulphurs (by sulphurs I mean forms) of the mundane life require to be purified, nay, perhaps extirpated" (APP ¶87); "Its end is that so-called annihilation bringing back into the Nothing which is before all things." (APP ¶90)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **The Key: lower desire opening into the heart life by ecstasy.**
  - "The Key to the Work, or into another life, the Key of the House of David, is obtained from the desire of the lower life, the generative, the fallen life, opening up into the heart life, or the medial life, or the celestial life, or the vegetable life, by exstasis." (APP ¶97)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **The upward draw by love.** The seed or paternal principle of this life is drawn up suddenly. Desire is redirected from below to above.
  - "The draw up is sudden and as at an instant; a sudden force of admiration does it" (APP ¶98)
  - Also: "The seed of this life, the paternal principle, is drawn up." (APP ¶98); "When the attraction is placed above instead of below a restoration of what was lost takes place and more also" (APP ¶98)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Fermenting one's individual Logos.** The core faith, one's own Logos, is worked through ("fermented") until it transmutes body, soul and spirit.
  - "The perfection of each man is to submit to have his fundamental faith, which is his individual Logos, perfectly elaborated --- i.e., fermented --- so as to transmute the whole being" (APP ¶101)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Light turned inward to find its center.** Light directed in toward its own center and origin finally wakes that center and makes it flash out.
  - "it is that light thrown in to find its own center and origin which awakens that center at last and causes it to coruscate" (APP ¶105)
  - interpreted as an operation (basis: the eye and body-light context of ¶105, antecedent uncertain); ATWOOD

- **The whole body enlisted.** The senses are turned against the sensuous medium in the blood. Members that serve the natural work also serve the super-essential one. Heart and brain are vessels; hand and eye are instruments.
  - "All the organs of the senses are brought to bear against the principle of sense or the sensuous medium in the blood; then the hands are taught to war and the fingers to fight" (APP ¶109)
  - Also: "All members that work here for the natural work, work also for the super-essential." (APP ¶109); "as the heart and brain are its vessels, so are the hand and eye its instruments and vessels also, the eye particularly" (APP ¶140)
  - stated; ATWOOD. "the hands are taught to war" is unexplained.

- **Loss of the understanding spirit, then a rally from a new center.** Cut off from its understanding spirit, the connate spirit's life drains away until it rallies by self-realization. Likewise, nature reduced to its last unit turns on its axis into new life.
  - "when the connate spirit is deprived of its understanding spirit, as is imaged in the Iliad after the death of Patroclus, and in the grief of Hercules for the loss of Hylas, life flows away, until by self-realization it rallies from a new center" (APP ¶109)
  - Also: "Nature reduced to the extremity of her last unit or monad turns upon her axis and rallies up into a new life" (APP ¶145)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Diving to the center in thought.** Anyone can reach the true center for moments if thought goes deep enough. Self-knowledge is consciousness's first contact with the ground of life.
  - "Every one can find in himself at moments the true center, if only the thought dives deep enough" (APP ¶115)
  - Also: "Self-knowledge is the first contact of consciousness in the ground of life." (APP ¶137)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Evaporating the vaporous vehicle.** The whole envelope must go before any truth emerges.
  - "The whole of the self-included vaporous vehicle of the soul must be evaporated before any truth is evolved." (APP ¶121)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Loosening a binding will by fermenting it.** A will that aims to loosen the will binding someone's life throws its purpose into that mind. This ferments the enclosing spirit, which opens, corrupts and dissolves, releasing the light that life was begotten from.
  - "Supposing I have a will that wishes to loosen your will, or rather the will that binds your life individually. I throw its purpose into your mind, and so ferment the whole including spirit of your life that it, the former, opens, corrupts, dissolves" (APP ¶126)
  - stated; ATWOOD. Whether this is an actual operation or an illustration is open.

- **Drawing power up through the feet.** In regeneration, power and life are drawn up through the feet like breathing or pumping. This light-substance may become operative and visible, or be returned to its source for a higher purpose.
  - "In the process of the Hermetic regeneration there is a drawing of power and life up through the feet which may be called breathing, pumping up of the rich essences of the spirit" (APP ¶158)
  - Also: "becomes through our organization into operation and visibility if permitted, or unless returned for a higher purpose to its fountain" (APP ¶158)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Restoring "the Efficient" to mind.** The active power now runs down into the generative life and is wasted. It must be regained and related to mind.
  - "The Efficient must be regained. That is, it is to be related to mind and not to the generative life." (APP ¶134)
  - Also: "The life is dislocated now; what ought to be up is down; it wastes itself." (APP ¶134)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Breaking the metal bond.** When it breaks, a preparatory "Elias spirit" is born. The text does not say what the metal bond is.
  - "When the metal bond is broken, the Elias spirit is born and his work is to prepare for something higher." (APP ¶139)
  - stated as sequence; ATWOOD

- **Passing the phantasmagoric region to the world's "solution".** After that region is passed, one enters the dissolved essence (the philosophers' mercury, Proteus). From there the inner vision is the same for everyone.
  - "after they have passed the phantasmagoric region and so far unriddled the sphinx of this world as to enter into its solution, i.e., in its dissolved essence which is the mercury of the Philosophers, Proteus" (APP ¶144)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Freedom of volition, then surrender.** Regeneration brings power to realize every act of will. In the higher Christ-life that self-will is given up to the Father's Will.
  - "In the Regeneration there is a wonderful birth of freedom to act every and realize every volition; this, so far as regards self-will, is given up under the Christ-life in its higher development and subjected to the Father's Will." (APP ¶151)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **"The handling of the Matter" through the hand.** The Matter flows out from the newborn consciousness through hand and fingers and ferments another life into the same consciousness. That life rises and raises the first in return.
  - "when the Matter flows forth exteriorly from the new-born consciousness, it passes out into the hand and flows through the fingers, and is able to ferment another life and bring it into the same consciousness" (APP ¶164)
  - Also: "this in its turn rises higher, and is able to react, raising again that other which raised it" (APP ¶164); "When one life being fermented throws its life to another equally fermented, a greater perfection is produced in the patient than was before in the agent who imparts it." (APP ¶42)
  - stated; ATWOOD. Reading "another life" as another person is interpreted.

- **The first experience of regeneration: contact with universal power.** The individual mind's contact with universal, efficient power ferments the sensory life and lives on its decline.
  - "for that experience is the initial of the regenerate life; that which ferments the sensorium of this life, and lives upon its decrease ultimately" (APP ¶175)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Rest.** The number seven stands for rest from the ferment.
  - "The great principle of the number seven is a Rest from the ferment or action of the Vitalizing or Divine principles." (APP ¶69)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Stages named by images.** The Second Principle is the Paradisiacal Life, lived "under the Cross". The philosophers' metals are the Ether's successive stages. The Aristaeus myth images an advanced stage.
  - "Living in the Second Principle means living the Paradisiacal Life" (APP ¶94)
  - Also: "The Metals of the philosophers are the ethereal metals; the Ether's progressive stages; its processes" (APP ¶180); "a myth which bears altogether on the Vital transmutation in an advanced stage" (APP ¶173)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Unstable forms in the lower development.** Self-contained spherical forms arise one after another and dissolve, because they lack the Light Essential.
  - "In the lower development of the Hermetic life, the quintessences successively form themselves, having each of them a spherical capacity and wholeness, but these world forms are unstable and, uninformed with the Light Essential, resolve and die." (APP ¶23)
  - stated; ATWOOD

## Impediments and dangers

- **Self-will: power turned diabolical or demoniacal.** Power gained and taken into self-will becomes evil. The Egyptian magicians drew only on their own strength.
  - "taken into the self-will and used accordingly it becomes diabolical" (APP ¶4)
  - Also: "else it breaks and becomes demoniacal" (APP ¶9); "Magicians, like the Egyptians of that day, drew only from their own strength, that was the way in which the kingdom of Egypt fell" (APP ¶5)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **The inner war: two wills, enemies and lures.** Wherever power arises there is conflict. Two wills ("magnets") compete for the soul, and the self-willed spirit tries to seize the mystery for itself. The combat recurs and is not finished in this world.
  - "In the work, wherever there is power there is a conflict: the multitudinous enemies try to rush in, the self-willed spirit endeavors to gain the mystery and offers all its lures in order to get the end for itself" (APP ¶56)
  - Also: "There is a war in the Work between the self-will and the Universal Will" (APP ¶92); "There are two magnets in man, i.e., two wills contending for man" (APP ¶124); "This war is the war between two principles of the self-will and the Divine Will." (APP ¶154); "the process, however, goes on over and over again; it is not concluded in this world" (APP ¶153)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **The self-life seizes the Divine Light instead of serving it.** Saturn traps the Light and craves more. Midas, keeping the gold-making life as his own possession instead of returning it to its source, hardens and starves.
  - "This life of ours, instead of administering to the Divine Life, appropriates it." (APP ¶70)
  - Also: "He first of all entraps the Light, and then he longs for more to feed that center which he has entrapped" (APP ¶72); "for want of returning the aurific substance of life into its source and living there, becomes fossilized and starved in the mere possession of that life" (APP ¶159)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Inner idolatry: giving the First Matter a form of one's own for a private end.** Shaping the humanly producible First Mater from one's own mind for a particular end is "the true idolatry" and the origin of talismanic magic. The "graven image" is an inner utterance fixed in the understanding and worshipped, far more dangerous than material idols.
  - "it is a taking the First Mater which is humanly producible, and forming it finitely and for any particular object or individual end" (APP ¶37)
  - Also: "in this consists the true idolatry against which the Bible so strongly protests, and that Magic which originated Talismans, Palladia, etc." (APP ¶37); "A graven image means a vocal utterance carved into the understanding spirit which conceives it by the desire of life" (APP ¶100); "the danger and sin of the latter is as nothing compared to those of the former, for in it was vitality and efficacy" (APP ¶100)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Setting up a self-center or making oneself a being: Lucifer's error.** Gifted people tend to set up self-centers. The will making itself into a being is "the evil": Lucifer stirred the central fire in his will and burned up the ether that was Heaven.
  - "The creating itself into a being is the evil." (APP ¶133)
  - Also: "the danger is the tendency in such gifted beings to set up self-centers" (APP ¶100); "the stirring up of the central fire in his will would inflame the vehicle of his life, and burn up the ether which was Heaven" (APP ¶133)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Stopping at intermediate gifts.** The work offers many states, objects and gifts. Stopping at any of them, or treating first-fruits as final, is idolatry, and stopping short is punished.
  - "you may stop in any of them; if you do, you commit idolatry" (APP ¶135)
  - Also: "The first-fruits, tough perfectly real, should not be accepted as permanent" (APP ¶74); "The Divine Art evolves various states of life and develops various possibilities, offering many objects and allurements to the will." (APP ¶133); "every stoppage of the will short of this is in its way punished" (APP ¶160); "unless you are wicked, and steal the first fruits of the spirit's offering" (APP ¶135)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Vulcan with Minerva.** When the stillness is stirred, the self-will can seize the Wisdom-life.
  - "when Vulcan appears in the same altar with Minerva the conjunction is ominous" (APP ¶82; QUOTED: "the older masters", glossed by Atwood)
  - Also: "of itself it needs nothing, but if it is stirred it is taken; what takes it is the self-will" (APP ¶82)
  - Atwood's gloss is stated. Which figure stands for which is interpreted; open.

- **Haste.** Breaking the seals of the book of life artificially to hurry the work is the chief danger. Ripley's "red poppies" mean the work burns up and must be begun again. Excess in religious striving is also self-will, and shows as spiritual pride, contempt, morbid self-anatomy, uncharity and odium theologicum.
  - "The danger of diving into the mystery of life through Alchemical seekings is that it tempts you to break the seals upon the book of life artificially in order to hasten the work, instead of waiting for God's will and pleasure about it." (APP ¶102)
  - Also: "Norton says, "Haste is the Devil's part"" (APP ¶103; QUOTED: Norton, endorsed); "There is a swiftness in the self-willed magnet working powerfully and to destruction if suffered to do so before its time." (APP ¶103); "Ripley warns against this under the similitude of red poppies --- the work gets all burnt up and the artist has to begin over again" (APP ¶103; QUOTED: Ripley, endorsed); "Be not righteous overmuch, says Solomon" (APP ¶110)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **The lowest life is full of defilement and temptation**, so entering it requires a pure will.
  - "that life is full of defilement and temptation" (APP ¶27)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **The evestrum: the soul's preset dream of its destiny.** It gives motives to a will that does not see beyond it, and is seldom passed except by God's grace.
  - "we may rise above this evestrum, as Paracelsus calls it" (APP ¶57)
  - Also: "I think that the soul comes into this body with the dream of its whole afterlife presented to it." (APP ¶57); "unless moved by God's grace (the Divine evestrum, relatively to man)" (APP ¶57)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **The phantastic region and the Chimaeras.** The analogical or visionary sphere (Regio phantastica, Evester) has only relative truth. As the seeking light enters the ether, the imaginative spirit throws up fantastic forms. All of them must be destroyed to reach the root of the seeking reason. Self-desire adulterates what is seen there.
  - "These Chimaeras have all to be destroyed in order to reach the simple Hypostasis or radix of the reason that seeks" (APP ¶171)
  - Also: "There is a certain truth in the analogical sphere, but it is only relative. The wise called it the Regio phantastica" (APP ¶105); "it is self-desire that adulterates" (APP ¶105); ""No one", says Vaughan, "enters into the Magian School but he wanders awhile in the region of Chimaeras"" (APP ¶171; QUOTED: Vaughan, endorsed); "the imaginative spirit is the maker of these images" (APP ¶171)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **The intellectual seeker's snare.** He uses the single ray of intellect to dive deeper, instead of letting the light convert the darkness of the self. The result is revelations "without clothing or realization", and darkness looking into light only darkens.
  - "they desire to dive deeply by the one ray of intellect rather than to transfuse its converting light and essence through the darkness of the selfhood on which it shines" (APP ¶114)
  - Also: "high revelations may be obtained in that way, intuitions even, but without clothing or realization" (APP ¶115); "It requires Light to throw Light upon Light; darkness looking in and endeavoring only darkens." (APP ¶118)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **An impure receiving medium; the soul's image defaced by selfhood.** Truth needs a pure receiving medium, and nature has none. The fall into selfhood has defaced the soul's image, so it neither reflects nor perceives truth until regenerated.
  - "everything depends on the purity of the receiving power" (APP ¶43)
  - Also: "the lapse of the human soul, the fall away of it into selfhood, which has defaced the image, so that it no longer reflects the truth nor perceives it until it has been regenerated" (APP ¶120)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Speaking from spirit-suggestions.** Voicing what spirits suggest gives them footing, and they possess and plague the soul.
  - "To utter from spirit-suggestions is a fearful thing; spirits get footing in the life through utterance" (APP ¶127)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **A bad will forced on someone being magnetized** can corrupt the natural purifying tendency, though not easily.
  - "you may corrupt it by introducing forcibly upon the patient a bad will, but it is not, naturally easy to do so" (APP ¶91)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Self-consciousness closing in after childhood.** Once the circle of individual life closes, free intercourse with Nature's spirit is shut in and self becomes the magnet.
  - "it is imprisoned in the self-consciousness, and self becomes the magnet" (APP ¶122)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Inversion: life now upside down and wasted downward.**
  - "Our present state is one of inversion." (APP ¶45)
  - Also: "in this life, we are upside down" (APP ¶125); "what ought to be up is down; it wastes itself" (APP ¶134)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **The halting motion of individual life.** Inner principles are bent off course as they come outward, and motion in this life runs in a line instead of returning.
  - "they are thrown into deviation in the course of evolution by the halting, obstructive movements of the law of individual life" (APP ¶112)
  - Also: "motion or action in this life is halting; it runs after something which it never catches" (APP ¶129)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **The work is not completed in this life.** Few perfect it, and it is not meant to be finished "till the time comes".
  - "the birth of this life prevents the complete work; it is not intended to be completed till the time comes" (APP ¶101)
  - stated; ATWOOD. What "the birth of this life" refers to is open.

## The result

- **The Philosopher's Stone: a real, concentrated essence of light.** It is the outflow of the regenerated life drawn to a focus, the true Form or Idea of Gold. It is a real thing born by spiritual generation, both objective and subjective.
  - "the Philosopher's Stone is the efflux of such a life, drawn to a focus and made manifest as a concrete Essence of Light, which Essence is the true Form or Idea of Gold" (APP ¶6)
  - Also: "The Philosopher's Stone is a real entity produced by spiritual generation; it is a real ens of light; it is both objective and subjective --- an actuality as well as a theory." (APP ¶93); "such an ens was the basis of the philosophic stone, a metaphysico-chemical birth" (APP ¶108)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **The Stone as the soul perfected.** It is also the lowest life perfected, and the whole regenerate life-force raised into head-consciousness.
  - "The soul in its metalline perfection is the philosopher's stone." (APP ¶181)
  - Also: "The soul in its perfection is the Christian philosopher's stone." (APP ¶181); "Everything is in the perfected Corner Stone, when the whole force of life regenerate is brought up into the head-consciousness" (APP ¶134); "the Stone of the Wise is the perfected lowest life" (APP ¶46)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Consciousness knows itself in its source, the Universal Center.** Since every being holds its own cause, touching one's First Cause means knowing the cause of all. True knowledge is lived contact of mind with mystery, the identity of knower and known.
  - "comes to know and feel itself in its own true Source, which is the Universal Center and source of all things" (APP ¶6)
  - Also: "if, by these alleged means the consciousness can be brought into contact with its First Cause, he will, in knowing It know the cause of all" (APP ¶6); "True Knowledge is experimental contact of mind with mystery; it is when the mind enters into the Universal consciously" (APP ¶75); "True Science is the contact identical of the thing knowing with the thing known, subject with object." (APP ¶171)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **The mind is related to the Universal Vitalizing Power and acts its will.**
  - "the mind becomes in that case related to the Universal Vitalizing Power, and so can act Its will" (APP ¶3)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **The soul is freed and the body becomes a husk or coat.** The medium between soul and body is changed. In the regenerate, the body is shot through with light like iron in a forge.
  - "that which is the medium between soul and body is changed, and the soul freed from the chains of corporeity, and the body is left as a mere husk" (APP ¶25)
  - Also: "These people put on their bodies as mere coats." (APP ¶25); "the body became as the iron in the forge, no longer visible as before, but penetrated through and swallowed up with the Light" (APP ¶105)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Invisible spirit made visible and bounded.**
  - "The physical problem as Kirchberger calls it (Theos. Correspondence, p. 168) is the making visible the invisible spirit." (APP ¶93; term QUOTED: Kirchberger, endorsed)
  - Also: "it is a taking of the infinite Light-nature into bound by hypostatic multiplication of its shadow or image" (APP ¶93)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Alchemical gold: the Paternal Light and aurific seed (the Golden Bough).** This Light springs up when reason consciously finds its roots in the higher Cause.
  - "The Gold Alchemical is the first conceived Paternal Light --- it is the seed metaphysical of mineral gold" (APP ¶20)
  - Also: "The Light which springs up in that alliance is the Divine aurific seed and true form of gold. That was the Golden Bough which Aeneas carried" (APP ¶152)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Life made circular and eternal.** Life now runs in a line. Returned by faith to its beginning, it closes the circle and is in Eternity. The tail-biting serpent is the image.
  - "when our life is returned in faith (for it is faith which reunites) to its beginning, it forms the circle and is in Eternity" (APP ¶9)
  - Also: "the serpent with his tail in his mouth is the chosen illustration of this reduction of the linear into the circular life" (APP ¶48)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **The self-willed life transmuted, passive and receptive.** Fed with light, the selfish life takes in the new life and is transmuted. Drawn up, the self-willed source becomes a passive feminine principle. The fallen body of the will becomes a ground and body for the Divine, by man's consent.
  - "the selfish Saturnine life in man when once it has passed into its new life, or rather admitted it, assimilates that life into itself and becomes itself transmuted" (APP ¶72)
  - Also: "this latter becomes transmuted into a passive feminine principle" (APP ¶98); "bring the self-willed opposition into subjection through man's consent and operation, in order that the original fallen body of the will in Lucifer may become a ground and body to the Divine instead of being apostate" (APP ¶104)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **More is restored than was lost.**
  - "When the attraction is placed above instead of below a restoration of what was lost takes place and more also" (APP ¶98)
  - Also: "for the second Adam is greater than the first" (APP ¶98)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **A true image of the Divine; the Divine Will is all.** The re-arisen spirit is a true manifestation of the Divine. Assimilation is completed and selfhood has nothing left to deface. The archetypal Light, once perceived, burns up all earlier images.
  - "When it re-arises it is a true manifestation, reflex and image of the Divine; that is the condition of the highest regeneration." (APP ¶87)
  - Also: "until the assimilation is perfected and the Divine Will is all" (APP ¶96); "the place of its manifestation is in the soul" (APP ¶96); "When the archetypal Light is perceived, it burns up all anterior manifestation, images and representations" (APP ¶106)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Light lodged in its causal center: evil thrown off and being reintegrated.** When the individual light of life sits in its causal center, adhering evil is thrown to the extremities and the threefold life is free to ascend. All life orders itself around the realized Rational Light.
  - "when the individual light of life is lodged in its causal center, the whole of the evil nature adhering is precipitated to the extremities, destroying the downward gravitating attraction or rather overcoming it, and leaving all the threefold life freedom of ascent" (APP ¶150)
  - Also: "All life moves orderly round the focus of that Alight when that Light is found or realized. This constitutes the re- integration of being, the manifestation of the **** in man." (APP ¶168)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Man becomes Nature's revealer and master, not her servant.** One who finds Wisdom sees the nature and virtues of all things. Entering "the Signature" clears the understanding to know things in their roots.
  - "he has a capacity in his own principle of being and life, when allied to God, to become, next Him, her revealer and master" (APP ¶120)
  - Also: "If you find the Wisdom, and coming out in the Spirit of Wisdom, look into the world, then you may see as Solomon did the nature and virtues of all things." (APP ¶67); "When an entrance is effected into the Signature, then there is a clearing of the understanding" (APP ¶63)
  - stated, except that linking the Signature with finding Wisdom is interpreted; ATWOOD

- **Wisdom for whoever receives the imparted spirit.** It wakes a consciousness related to the universal.
  - "Qui capit, ille sapit is another, and refers to the wisdom imparted by the reception of the Afflatus, since it awakens in him a consciousness related to the universal" (APP ¶41)
  - stated; ATWOOD, glossing an old alchemical saying

- **Entry into the center of life, where all who enter agree.** The same revelation is given to each.
  - "an entrance into the center of the substantial life which every creature is seeking" (APP ¶113)
  - Also: "For this truth is at once a unity and an universal." (APP ¶113)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **The new nature acts through the person as "He".**
  - "when this nature is born in us it asserts itself (or rather, I should say, He asserts Himself, for it is more that It) through us" (APP ¶51)
  - stated wording. Who "He" is remains open; ATWOOD

- **Union with God: sonship and marriage.**
  - "The Christian scheme is the bringing of the whole of our life into the Sonship by regeneration; it is an eternal marriage-union between man and God in Christ" (APP ¶79)
  - stated; ATWOOD

## Principles (general laws of the work)

- **What alchemy is.** Divine chemistry and the transmutation of life. Holy Alchemy, through which man is brought into relation with God. The seeking of Sophia in the mind. True alchemy is the physical side of theosophy.
  - "Alchemy is Divine Chemistry, and the transmutation of Life" (APP ¶25)
  - Also: "It is through this Art that man is brought into relationship with God, hence it is called by the Adepts Holy Alchemy" (APP ¶29); "Alchemy is philosophy; it is the philosophy, the seeking of The Sophia in the mind." (APP ¶183); "True Alchemy (not that which the world has run after without finding) is related to Theosophy on its physical side" (APP ¶78); "The Hermetic Philosophy is a process of experimentation into the Universal Spirit through man" (APP ¶53)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Fermentation is the governing image, and it ascends.** Unlike ordinary generation, which keeps the offspring level with the parent, fermentation produces something higher each time. The receiver ends more perfected than the giver, and every manifestation holds the seed of a higher one.
  - "The word fermentation conveys an idea of the perfecting principle and of the possibility of transmutation beyond any other word" (APP ¶27)
  - Also: "spiritual generation is according to the fermentative law; by fermentation of its own light it keeps on rising, until it reaches the perfection of its law. It is an ascending, not a descending, process." (APP ¶132); "a greater perfection is produced in the patient than was before in the agent who imparts it. That is the law of progression of the Vital force" (APP ¶42); "Every manifestation contains the promise of another superior to it" (APP ¶148)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Agent and patient act on each other.**
  - "The whole work is an action of agent on patient, and the reaction of patient, advancing, on agent." (APP ¶111)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Correspondence of microcosm and macrocosm.** Man contains the whole. Each of his levels relates him to the same level outside, and his three centers map onto the three kingdoms.
  - "man, being a microcosm, comprehends and represents the whole and each" (APP ¶35)
  - Also: "wheresoever we stand ourselves, there is the relation of the outward life to us itself formed" (APP ¶59); "The whole image of the macrocosm is formed, and has consequently its typical representation, in the Microcosm" (APP ¶55)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Three lives and three centers.** Terrestrial: head, sensuous, animal. Celestial: heart and solar plexus, emotional, vegetable, medial. Infernal: lumbar, voluntary, mineral.
  - "There are three Lives to be represented. The Terrestrial, the Celestial, the Infernal." (APP ¶32)
  - Also: "The Infernal is the voluntary agent in life, and represents the Mineral Kingdom. In man it resides in the lumbar region." (APP ¶33); "There are three microcosmic centers in man --- the eye of the Divine, animal rational head life; the heart of the celestial medial vegetable life; the lumbar region of the inferior mineral life." (APP ¶95); "there is a power of entering into three modes of consciousness: either in the sensible, the perceptive, or the power life; this last conjoined to the other two makes them efficient" (APP ¶45)
  - stated; ATWOOD. How the triads map onto each other is open.

- **Salt, Mercury and Sulphur are principles, not physical substances.** In the blood's vital force they are Attraction, Circulation and Repulsion. The alchemical names refer to principles that become qualities only in spirit.
  - "There are three principles in this force, Attraction, Circulation, Repulsion --- the Alchemical Salt, Mercury, Sulphur." (APP ¶24)
  - Also: "the Sulphur, the Salt and the Mercury; the water, the air, the fire, etc., relates to principles rather than qualities: in the spirit they become qualities" (APP ¶81); "Thought qualitates spirit, and spiritual experiences bring the qualities produced into manifestation, into esse from posse." (APP ¶81)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **The Work imitates Divine manifestation.** Will arises in nothing to bring it into something so that it can behold itself. Will subsists in one will, then in two, exists in a third, and becomes self-assertion.
  - "This is the order imaged out in the Hermetic Work, which is in fact an imitation of the Divine." (APP ¶73)
  - Also: ""An Eternal Will arises in the No Thing to introduce the No Thing into Something, that the Will might feel and find and behold itself"" (APP ¶73; QUOTED: Behmen, endorsed); "It subsists first in one will, then in two wills, and exists in the third; it then becomes a self-assertion." (APP ¶86)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Perpendicular is Divine, transverse is contrary.**
  - "The perpendicular line is the Divine line in all things; the transverse line is the contrary; the two united form a cross +." (APP ¶13)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **The symbols are real and the old myths are Hermetic.** The disguising symbols point to a reality and are more literally true than thought. Atwood holds the Hermetic reading to be the only true key to the myths, Homer and Virgil (the Georgics as "spiritual agriculture", Aeneid VI especially).
  - "The Alchemical symbols, phrases, metaphors, which so disguise the art are more than what they seem; they point out a reality" (APP ¶61)
  - Also: "I am perfectly sure that the Hermetic is the only true key to the old myths, and to Homer's Iliad and Odyssey" (APP ¶157); "the Georgic's are an allegory all through, an analogical instruction in celestial and spiritual agriculture" (APP ¶157); "The Walls of Troy are a figure (and were meant by the poets and traditionalists to be so)" (APP ¶49)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **The a priori method.** Knowledge from the inner cause outranks induction. Once found, it proves itself outwardly by effects. The only valid objection to it is the soul's fall into selfhood.
  - "The a priori method is the true one for the discovery of the first truths" (APP ¶120)
  - Also: "The subjective can prove itself, has proved itself, to the objective; the objective never can prove itself by originating any effects." (APP ¶120); "The moment men begin to reduce the ontological ground taken by Adepts to physical science they begin to twaddle." (APP ¶30)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **The spirit's own language.** Ordinary language cannot fully express the inner life. It becomes clearer as thought works the inner experience out.
  - "The language of the spirit itself only can clearly express and reveal its own life." (APP ¶111)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Art beyond nature.** The Art takes nature as given and carries her beyond her created state. It exists because there is art in the Divine world, and it is revealed to men undergoing regeneration.
  - "Alchemy goes beyond nature; it takes her up and advances her beyond her created it and in this world." (APP ¶155)
  - Also: "Art, then, what nature left in hand doth take, And out of one a twofold work doth make." (APP ¶156, verse, cited without attribution); "If there were no art in the Divine world there would be none in this. The mystery is an art revealed to regenerating men, to advance them on to perfection" (APP ¶146)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **The medial center comes with its law, an impulse toward regeneration.** Spirit grows from spirit-seed as a plant grows from its seed.
  - "You can ’t get the medial center, the anima media, either of man or nature without its law, and that law is an impulse towards rectification and regeneration." (APP ¶147)
  - Also: "As the seed of a natural object to its nature, so is the seed of spirit to spirit." (APP ¶147)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **The two ethers and the three spheres.** The mundane Ether is full of false forms; the pure Ether is linked to the supersensual life. Above the natural ether (where passions and images rise) lies the pure Ether of the moral faculties, and above that the Divine Light region.
  - "The Ether is two-fold: the mundane, which is full of the false forms; the pure, which is not of this world but is connected with the supersensual life." (APP ¶64)
  - Also: "The higher faculties, the moral nature, with their results, are related to the pure Ether, which overlies the passion sphere. Above that is the Divine Light region" (APP ¶170)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Fallen nature gives a distorted image.** Nature has no pure medium for conceiving truth. Since the Fall it no longer gives a true echo of God's voice, and the Fall began spiritually, well behind its effects.
  - "There is no such thing as truth absolute in nature, because she has no true conceptive vehicle" (APP ¶43)
  - Also: "The present form of Nature is a fall off from the true representation, it gives a distorting shadow of the true." (APP ¶161); "The birth into this life is a continuous image o the Fall; the Fall was not at first sensible, but spiritual" (APP ¶121)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Numbers order the creative law and are known through theurgic art**, where spirit is added to spirit and multiplied.
  - "through their personal representation in theurgic art, by which spirit is added to spirit and multiplied as offspring, producing points, planes, cubes, which being again conjoined, have their confluence in materiality" (APP ¶107)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Grammar mirrors the operations of spirit.**
  - "will is the substantive or noun, love the qualitative adjective form; their union coming into self-expression is the verb affirmative, I am" (APP ¶109)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Weight is the analogue of Will.** Not elaborated.
  - "Weight is the analogue of Will." (APP ¶123)
  - stated; ATWOOD

- **Emanation becoming solid.** The darkness is the emanation from superstantial Light or Will, and what was flat becomes cubic by multiplying into itself.
  - "The whole doctrine of the ontological infolding is emanation; emanation from the superstantial Light or will." (APP ¶66)
  - Also: "It is Mathesis becoming geometric." (APP ¶66)
  - stated; ATWOOD. Its bearing on practice is not stated.

- **The downward-born image (Eve).** The image thrown forth, in which Adam saw his own soul, attracts the soul and, united with it, brings the ideal forth as actual offspring.
  - "which image of perfect beauty attracts the soul, and united to it brings forth the ideal into an actual offspring" (APP ¶18)
  - stated; ATWOOD. Its bearing on practice is not stated.

- **Three principles developed in the same way, the fourth by retrogression.**
  - "The third principle is developed in like manner. The fourth contrariwise; it is a procreation of the third through the second --- a retrogression." (APP ¶39)
  - stated; ATWOOD. The numbering is unexplained (see Open questions).

## Open questions

- **What is the "third life": the starting point, or the goal?** One reading: it is the fallen lowest life man is already in, which is released and raised (¶2 "the release or setting free the third or lowest life"; ¶35 "this third life has to be raised up"; ¶44, ¶59). The other: it is the state reached after two fermentations (¶3 "that third life which moves in and with the creative Essence"; ¶22 the seeker "in the third life" wears the Golden Fleece). These may be the same life before and after release. The text does not settle it.
- **What or who is "its similar" / "another life" that ferments?** One reading: another person. Basis: ¶41 "He who imparts the spirit to another", ¶42 agent and patient, ¶126 "I throw its purpose into your mind", ¶164 the Matter passing through the hand to "another life" that raises the first in return, the mesmerism passages ¶13, ¶73, ¶91. The other: the same life acting on itself, or another level within one person. Basis: ¶2 "by the fermentation of itself", ¶27 "in and by its own Light", ¶183 active, passive and resultant principles "of the life", ¶163 two pairs of eyes. ¶162 ("the agent in the patient, or the patient in the agent") fits either. ¶126 begins "Supposing", so it may be an illustration rather than a procedure.
- **Is becoming "creative" the goal or forbidden?** ¶7–8 say mind "becomes creative and immortalized". ¶35 forbids aspiring to the head life, "since if he were there he would become Creative --- he would be taking to himself the Divine Will". ¶151 has the power of realizing every volition given up to the Father's Will. ¶7–8 may describe the law of Will in higher states, not man's aim.
- **The head life: highest or sensuous?** ¶32 calls the Terrestrial head life "the sensuous". ¶35 says it typifies the highest life man must not aspire to. ¶134 places the perfected Stone in "head-consciousness", and ¶140 has every member serve "the law of the head". ¶45 lists three triads (sensible, perceptive, power; head, medial, divine) without equating them.
- **The lumbar will-power "sent back" (¶11).** It may be reversed upward (¶33 voluntary life in the lumbar region; ¶35 reversal of the lives), or returned to its source (¶9). Unexplained.
- **"The Salt, the will, opens it secondfold" (¶182).** Possible senses: the will opens the life to its second (medial) level; the will performs both dissolutions; or the will unfolds the life in a second manner. The sentences that follow describe two dissolutions without naming the will.
- **Perpendicular and horizontal magnetism (¶13, ¶173).** The text says vertical dissolves and frees the soul, horizontal fixes. It does not say whether these are literal mesmeric passes or inner directions. The mesmerism passages (¶73 "by the perpendicular or vertical passes") favour some outward correlate, while ¶73 calls mesmerism "a very external way --- an imitation".
- **Vulcan and Minerva (¶82).** One reading: Vulcan (Motion, ¶129) is the stirring, Minerva the Wisdom-life, and the danger is that once stirred it is taken by self-will. Basis: ¶82's gloss "what takes it is the self-will". The text does not say which figure is which, and stirring is both enjoined and called ominous.
- **"There are two Causes and one Mother in the Work" (¶73).** Unexplained beyond a pointer to Oedipus ("Qui capit, ille sapit"). A link to the two wills (¶92, ¶94) or to "one will, then in two wills, and exists in the third" (¶86) is speculative.
- **"The fourth contrariwise; it is a procreation of the third through the second --- a retrogression" (¶39).** It may be a separate numbered series of principles, or the numbered lives (third = mineral, second = medial?). Not identified.
- **"ignis et Azoth tibi sufficient" (¶131).** Fire and Azoth may be the only means the freed spirit needs, or names for the freed spirit itself. Unglossed.
- **Golden Bough (¶152, ¶168, ¶171).** It is identified with the aurific seed or Light (¶152), but it is also an offering made "By propitiation" and a "talisman" for passing Tartarus. What the propitiatory offering consists of in practice is not said.
- **The inner combat (¶153).** The text does not say which side's rational light loses its "understanding essence" and goes forth "to avenge it and die". ¶109's parallel (the connate spirit deprived of its understanding spirit, then rallying) suggests the self side, but this is not stated.
- **"the birth of this life prevents the complete work" (¶101).** It may mean the present natural life stands in the way, or that the new birth itself marks a limit. Either fits "not intended to be completed till the time comes".
- **Green and purple, and "benedicta viriditas" (¶119).** The nearest antecedent makes the purple ripened Life the benedicta viriditas. The passage's main subject, green vegetative life, makes the green the viriditas. The text also says the Golden Fleece is "of a purplish luster" (¶22).
- **"they renounce a word" (¶60).** Possibly "world", given that the passage is about those "in this world" giving up the head life. As printed, it could be a word or vow.
- **"misconceive this Evester ... and having a see things pretty truly" (¶105).** The text is corrupt. It seems that visionaries can still see fairly truly "if they continue faithful of purpose without much self-desire". Words may be missing.
- **"the manifestation of the **** in man" (¶168).** A word is suppressed in the source and its content is unknown.
- **"you get into the intuitional (divine)" (¶176).** Either a second, divine intuitional level, or a slip for "the divine" (cf. ¶182 "Dissolve the medial and you reach to the divine").
- **"represented personally produces a universal work" (¶183).** Each principle may be embodied in a distinct person, or realized in the individual's own person. Basis: ¶182 "Each life must have its key and representative"; ¶164 another life.
- **"contrition, live, purity" (¶37).** "live" is probably a misprint for "love" in this list of heart-emotions, but it is printed "live". Similarly "the epigastric region and the heat" (¶32), possibly "heart", given the parallel with the other bodily seats and ¶95's heart center. Also "we are not immoral" (¶9), possibly "immortal", given the contrast with the circle that "is in Eternity".
- **"He asserts Himself" (¶51).** Who "He" is goes unnamed: a divine person (cf. ¶46, ¶60 "our Lord"), or a personification of the nature born in us.
- **"the essential fold which is of the substance of light" (¶158).** "fold" is unexplained, possibly "gold" (cf. ¶152 "true form of gold").
