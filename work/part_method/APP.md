# Method synthesis — Appendix: Table Talk and Memorabilia (later layer)

**Layer note.** The Appendix ("Table Talk and Memorabilia of Mary Anne Atwood", begun August 1860) is a later layer than the 1850 book: numbered aphorisms (1)–(181) plus unnumbered closing notes, not a continuous argument. Nearly all is ATWOOD's own voice; cited authorities are tagged where they occur. Refs are `APP ¶n`. The underlying summary records no passage comparing these notes with the 1850 book; see Tensions.

## Subject

1. **The lowest ("third", mineral) life that fallen man now lives; the Art perfects it.** "The Hermetic Art is a perfectioning of the third or lowest or the mineral life in man" (¶44); "Man is fallen; he is in the third or lowest life" (¶35). stated.
2. **One's present life, petrified, taken as it is.** "life in each is as a stone in this first being" (¶132). stated.
3. **The impure vital spirit, always in chaos, needing rebirth.** "the chaos of te Vital Spirit (which always is in chaos, i.e., always coming to be, never is, and whilst in this life wants the rebirth)" (¶165). stated.
4. **Seat: body, blood, members.** "The process takes place in and through the human body in the blood" (¶6); the limbs "are a laboratory that is of and for the spirit to work in" (¶16). stated.
5. **The impure vital atmosphere around each person**, imaging thoughts and desires, its density seen as darkness or brightness. "The whole process of the Mystery was intended to purify the impure vital atmosphere which surrounds each individual life." (¶73). stated.
6. **The soul's vaporous vehicle**, taken on at conception: "it obscures the pure paternal light of life --- the generative light, in order to bring it out into sense" (¶121). stated.
7. **A hidden gold-seed in that life** (Chain 7): "the rational ferment, the pure aurific seed of Light, is hidden in this life of ours" (¶35); "hidden in the voluntary contrariation" (¶125). stated.
8. **Salt, the will-substance, bound "in the lowest region, the generative mineral life"** (¶77). stated.
9. **"The fire": one's chaotic will, "the basis of our microcosmic recreation"** (¶97). stated; glossing Kirchberger.
10. **The Philosophic Matter: light drawn out through anguish; the body's entelechy bearing spirit; the One Thing.** "alight extracted through anguish from Life" (¶84); "The human soul is more than a mere resultant of body; it is its ultimate perfection, for it is a mesothesis between two spheres." (¶178). stated; "entelechy" unglossed.
11. **The First Matter found by going back in consciousness, not on earth.** "go back in consciousness. It is immediately antecedent to the first consciousness." (¶137); "The pure matter of the philosophers is not to be found on earth" (¶43; QUOTED: Raymond Lully, endorsed). Plotinus's "recessure of reason behind itself" (¶137) is "merely the metaphysic of it". stated.
12. **Proteus, universal life before individuality**, hard to find, last disguised as a tree "identical with that bearing the Golden Bough" (¶171). stated.
13. **The Universal Ether**: "a scrap of that ether shut in at our birth-conception" (¶119). stated.
14. **The magical earth at one's own center**, the pure earth of the vegetable life that begins Regeneration; the mineral earth "belongs to the lower life and to the center of the globe" (¶89). Eirenaeus's Mercury "whose center it can never find but by discovering its own" (¶89; QUOTED: Eirenaeus, glossed). stated.
15. **The universal matter "is obtainable at the focus of either center"** (¶95). stated.
16. **The Hermetic sepulcher**, vehicle of the dead Divine Life and its own death (¶19). stated.

## Agent

1. **The Universal Spirit, drawn by the individual.** "In the Art the individual draws the Universal." (¶29); "a particular spirit (to use Vaughan's words) is allied to the universal" (¶53). stated.
2. **A like life as ferment; both dissolve, a third arises.** "The fire of the natural life entering into and fermenting the natural fire --- the same life in another --- opens the last, and develops and excites and sets free the celestial Life and Light --- that is one main principle." (¶38); "both will be reproduced in a third" (¶132). stated; "another" open (Tensions 2).
3. **Will, the only substance; what Will bound, Will unbinds.** "Everything is in Will. All in the beginning is Will" (¶86); "that which Will has bound, Will only can unbind" (¶125). stated.
4. **The Universal Will (Wisdom) as agent on the self-will as patient.** "The Universal Will being represented as agent dissolves the false forms in the self-will represented as patient." (¶94); "first destroying them, and then reproducing them in a transmuted form" (¶92). stated.
5. **Love, the pull from above.** "it is love which attracts. Nothing else could move that principle from its self-conscious center into self-oblivion." (¶98). stated.
6. **Emotion and conscience as ferment.** "conscience strikes upon the self-life, and, when it does so, effectually ferments it and evolves from it a third form of life, a new birth of life" (¶80); "contrition, live, purity, mercy, humility, are such emotions" (¶37). stated.
7. **Light, chiefly the Rational Light, under its law (Wisdom), moving through Love.** "Alchemy is a process of fermenting the vital Spirit in and by its own Light." (¶27); "The Rational Light is the strongest of all lights, and overcomes every other." (¶168); the law of Light "must move through Love in the will", "else it breaks and becomes demoniacal" (¶9). stated.
8. **Sulphur, the hidden masculine ferment, perfects the passive matter.** "you must set free the masculine ferment, the sulphur or divine ferment (which is doubly hidden and beyond in this life of ours) to effect the perfection of the matter" (¶182). stated.
9. **Mind as mover, Vulcan its instrument; a spirit-man moves the Ether.** "Vulcan is not the fire, the ignis Philosophorum, but the stirrer up of the fire." (¶129); "There is a spirit-man within the natural man and his work is to move the Ether." (¶139). stated.
10. **The spirit teaches and, freed, works its own art.** "The spirit teaches its own art, and according as it is obeyed the artist goes on developing the way" (¶40); "The spirit freed works by itself, by its own law, ignis et Azoth tibi sufficient; the wonder must be to see it working its miracle." 11. **Divine Will through voice and utterance.** "the Divine Will makes use of the human voice as an organ for conducting the Light, through the fire" (¶97; QUOTED: Kirchberger, endorsed: "It strikes me that he is right in this"); "If it should speak in us it will work its own change in us" (¶88). stated.

## Operator

1. **Pure will and intention before entering the lowest life consciously.** "it is therefore necessary, before going into it consciously, that the will and purpose be perfectly pure and simple in this return of the reason to its First Cause" (¶27); "all depends on the willing and the intention" (¶35). stated.
2. **The will must serve God's law.** "Power thus gained is only legitimate, just and righteous when the will subserves the commandment and law of God; taken into the self-will and used accordingly it becomes diabolical" (¶4); the Egyptian magicians "drew only from their own strength" (¶5). stated.
3. **Passive surrender; entire self-devotion to begin.** "given up in passivity to the Divine Will" (¶79); "nothing but entire self-devotion is of any use even to begin with" (¶90). stated.
4. **Seek the Universal first, singly.** "Seek ye first the Kingdom of God and His righteousness" (¶134) "is really the shortest as well as the truest road"; "If you seek a finite end first, i.e., before you seek the Universal --- the Kingdom of God --- which you are ordered to do, you will have to give up the former at every stage" (¶135). stated; applying Scripture.
5. **Act from the medial life (faith), not the head; do not aspire to the creative life.** "We have no right to be efficient in the present head life; our business is to be efficient in the medial life --- in faith; then we are efficient for the life immediately over us --- the divine." (¶45); "if he were there he would become Creative --- he would be taking to himself the Divine Will" (¶35); the regenerate "have given up the Head life or the animal, the rational life" (¶60). stated.
6. **Humility; reason informed by love; the faculty used decides what is found.** "the ontological quality of the faculty you seek with determines what you find" (¶170); "To the true seeker after truth in humility, not in pride, it responds." (¶116). stated.
7. **Patience and measure.** "it is the duty of man's will to follow God's Will as He, according to His time and measure, opens the way" (¶110); "Festina lente, Ne quid nimis, are sayings very applicable to the work" (¶110). stated.
8. **Lived experience, not theory.** "you must have the Universal developed in you before you can understand the Universal" (¶65); "Alchemy can be fully understood only by those who have entered on and are working the philosophic process" (¶181). stated.
9. **Two kinds of seeker.** The passive, loving "are worked at every surface of the Spirit, and this takes time; for doing is more tedious than seeing, but then it is surer" (¶115); "Intellectual seekers get quickly deeper, but have more to overcome." (¶115). stated.
10. **Temperament.** Mars/Venus: "their knowledge is by experience, not perception or intuition, which are the property of Mercury and Jupiter" (¶113), who "cannot feel as well, or realize without the voluntary dominance of Saturn and Luna" (¶113). stated.
11. **Spiritual guidance, never human initiation; tools and words found within.** "You are always taken down there by spiritual guides; it is never human initiation, that" (¶126); "The Initiated moved one another on by words of power." (¶100), found "in the subjective, the fundamental life" (¶100); "The true tools also may be found on the way in; they will be given one after another as they are wanted" (¶100). stated.
12. **Reverence and restraint in speech.** "We have no business whatsoever to speak The Name." (¶88); "one cannot venture to speak of it in ordinary language" (¶58); "I don't think you can think into such spheres of life without moving them, or being moved by them" (¶85). stated.
13. **Renounce first-fruits and offered riches.** "they must be sacrificed for the sake of the promise they contain, would he fulfill the Divine purpose and not his own" (¶74); "craving the riches of the whole world set before him, gives them up" (¶158). stated.

## Procedure and stages

### The whole
1. **Ferment, purify, dissolve, regenerate under a reversed law.** "Alchemy is the art of fermenting the human vital spirit in order to purify it and finally to dissolve it, so that the principle be reconstructed through a regeneration." (¶68); "a regeneration of the dissolved separated elements, under a reversed law" (¶7; QUOTED: "the Hermeticists", endorsed). stated.
2. **Reverse the natural order of the lives; translate consciousness to its source.** "The aim in the Work is to reverse the whole action of these lives in their natural order." (¶35); "this third life has to be raised up and born through the medial or celestial life, and becomes receptive of the Divine" (¶35). stated.
3. **Repeated over and over, gradual but progressive**; the disordered Hermetic texts describe different repetitions (¶17). "it is a successive giving up of itself to be bettered on the part of Will" (¶84); "each fermented spirit advances upon its origin" (¶111); "born over Andover again" (¶90). stated.
4. **Ascent through spheres, no standing still.** "a passage of life through various spheres of being, in any of which the will may rest, but every one of which presents a law of progress at its development which should be pursued to the end --- the Divine manifestation" (¶160). stated.
5. **An inquisition into life.** "The Hermetic Process is a regular inquisition into life. There is motion present with interrogation." (¶138); "The Art is a question put to Nature respecting the first cause" (¶48). stated.

### Reconstructed order
No single sequence is given. Two frames, each stated separately, are aligned here to supply a skeleton: two fermentations "by its similar" (¶2–3) and two dissolutions (¶182, ¶176). Their alignment (common trance = medial life; third life = the divine) is interpreted. Assigning other operations to a stage is interpreted from their vocabulary (medial / third life; first / later). All operations stated unless marked.

**0. Preparation (stated prerequisite).** Find the intention; purify the will (Operator 1–4). "The difficulty is to find this principium, the intention" (¶35).

**1. Set the spirit in motion (stated first).** "The first step in this Philosophy is to create motion in the spirit." (¶130).

**2. First fermentation: dissolve the sensuous medium; enter the medial life (stated frame).** "this present life may be led through the clairvoyant life into it (that is the wonderful part of it) by the fermentation of itself" (¶2); "this life being fermented by this life, its similar, leads into the common trance" (¶3); "Dissolve the sensuous medium and you have the second or medial life." (¶182); "You cut off the sensuous life and get into the intuitional" (¶176). Placed here by interpretation:
- *Reversing circulation in the blood by repeated contritions.* "reversing the circulatory order, so that, the sensible medium becoming occult, the inner source of its vitality is awakened, and the consciousness at the same time being drawn centrally" (¶6); "What is really changed is the magnetic attraction; the medial spirit is changed; life is attracted in, instead of out." (¶24).
- *The Key: lower desire opening into the heart life by ecstasy.* "The Key to the Work, or into another life, the Key of the House of David, is obtained from the desire of the lower life, the generative, the fallen life, opening up into the heart life, or the medial life, or the celestial life, or the vegetable life, by exstasis." (¶97).
- *The sudden draw up by love.* "The draw up is sudden and as at an instant; a sudden force of admiration does it" (¶98).
- *Raising Salt or Sulphur into the medial region as a graft.* "must be translated or drawn up into the medial life" (¶77); "the Salt thus transplanted becomes Light and grows" (¶77); "It is brought up like a graft" (¶125). The Troy-cattle parenthesis (¶77) maps here only by interpretation.
- *Lumbar will-power "sent back".* "There is a time when the power, the vital or willing-power, is seated in the lumbar region and is sent back." (¶11). stated as stage; meaning interpreted (basis ¶33, ¶35).
- *Satiating Saturn with Rational Light.* "Saturn or the self-willed life can only be overcome in the process by satiation; he must be given from without plenty "of the honey of the bees", the aurific seed, the Rational Light." (¶72).

## Tensions and open questions

**Relation to the 1850 book.** The summary records no passage in which the Appendix cites, revises or contrasts the 1850 book, so no stated difference between the layers can be asserted. Observable from this file alone: the Appendix is almost wholly in Atwood's own voice, cites authorities mostly with endorsement (Plotinus's account is "merely the metaphysic of it", ¶137; Van Helmont speaks "in a very superficial way", ¶157), and states bodily and mesmeric mechanics (blood, lumbar region, feet, hand, perpendicular and horizontal magnetism) in plain terms. Whether that develops or restates the 1850 method cannot be decided here.

1. **"Third life": start or goal?** Fallen man "is in the third or lowest life" (¶35), to be released and raised (¶2, ¶44, ¶59); yet the second fermentation "leads into that third life which moves in and with the creative Essence" (¶3) and the Golden Fleece is worn "in the third life" (¶22). Possibly one life before and after release.
2. **"Its similar" / "another life": another person or oneself?** Another person: ¶41 "He who imparts the spirit to another", ¶42, ¶126 "I throw its purpose into your mind", ¶164, the mesmerism passages (¶13, ¶73, ¶91). The same life or another level within one person: ¶2 "by the fermentation of itself", ¶27 "in and by its own Light", ¶183 principles "of the life" evolved separately, ¶163 two pairs of eyes. ¶162 fits either; ¶126 begins "Supposing". ¶183 "represented personally produces a universal work" may mean distinct persons or one's own (cf. ¶182 "Each life must have its key and representative").
3. **Becoming creative: goal or forbidden?** ¶7–8: mind "becomes creative and immortalized"; ¶35 forbids the head life "since if he were there he would become Creative"; ¶151 gives realized volition up to the Father's Will. ¶7–8 may state the law of Will in higher states, not man's aim.
4. **Head life: highest or sensuous?** ¶32 "the sensuous"; ¶35 typifies the highest life not to be aspired to; ¶134 Stone in "head-consciousness"; ¶140 "the law of the head"; ¶45 two triads unequated.
5. **Perpendicular and horizontal magnetism (¶13, ¶173): passes or inner directions?** ¶73 "by the perpendicular or vertical passes" favours an outward correlate; ¶73 also calls mesmerism "a very external way --- an imitation".
6. **Golden Bough: the Light itself or an offering?** Identified with the aurific seed (¶152), yet carried "By propitiation" and as "his talisman" (¶168, ¶171). The offering's practical content is unsaid.
7. **Vulcan and Minerva (¶82).** Stirring is enjoined ("seek to stir up the Motion") and called ominous; which figure is which is unstated.
8. **"The Salt, the will, opens it secondfold" (¶182).** Opens the life to its second level; performs both dissolutions; or unfolds it in a second manner.
9. **Lumbar will-power "sent back" (¶11).** Upward reversal (¶33, ¶35) or return to source (¶9).
10. **"There are two Causes and one Mother in the Work" (¶73).** Unexplained beyond a pointer to Oedipus; links to ¶92/¶94 or ¶86 are speculative.
11. **"The fourth contrariwise" (¶39).** A separate series of principles, or the numbered lives.
12. **"ignis et Azoth tibi sufficient" (¶131).** Means the freed spirit needs, or names for it.
13. **The inner combat (¶153).** Which side's rational light loses its "understanding essence" and goes "to avenge it and die" is unstated; ¶109 suggests the self side.
14. **"the birth of this life prevents the complete work" (¶101).** The natural life as obstacle, or the new birth as limit.
15. **Green, purple, benedicta viriditas (¶119).** Nearest antecedent: purple; passage subject: green; the Fleece is "of a purplish luster" (¶22).
16. **"you get into the intuitional (divine)" (¶176).** A second divine level, or a slip for "the divine" (cf. ¶182).
17. **"He asserts Himself" (¶51).** A divine person (cf. ¶46, ¶60 "our Lord") or a personification.
18. **Textual uncertainties.** "they renounce a word" (¶60), possibly "world"; "having a see things pretty truly" (¶105), corrupt; "the manifestation of the **** in man" (¶168), word suppressed; "contrition, live, purity" (¶37), probably "love"; "the epigastric region and the heat" (¶32), possibly "heart"; "we are not immoral" (¶9), possibly "immortal"; "the essential fold which is of the substance of light" (¶158), possibly "gold".
