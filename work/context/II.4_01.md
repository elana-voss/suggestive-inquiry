(from II.3_04)

[II.3 ¶56] We omit many things here relating to the mystical death and regeneration, which may be better
understood when we come to treat of the manifestation of the Philosophic Subject; adding merely at
present, in conclusion from our doctors, that the grand perfection of their Art was to multiply the
Prester and place him in the most supreme Ether, which is that Augean palace already prepared for him
in the beginning; where, as in a suitable habitation, he abides shining, not burning as below, or
wrathful; but vital, calm, transmuting, recreating, and no longer a Consuming Fire.
Intellige in scientia et sepias intellignetia: exprerire in illis, et investiga illa, et nota, et cogita, et
imaginare, et statue rem integritate et fac sedere Creatorm in throno suo (62).
