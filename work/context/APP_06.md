[APP ¶129] (134) The Vulcan of the Alchemists is Motion. Why Vulcan is represented as lame in the Myths is
because motion or action in this life is halting; it runs after something which it never catches; it runs in
a line, keeping on without returning, circularly into its own principle; in this life it is not meant to do
so, for nothing here contains its own principle in itself. Vulcan is not the fire, the ignis Philosophorum,
but the stirrer up of the fire.
