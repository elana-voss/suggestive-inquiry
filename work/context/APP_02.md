[APP ¶35] We must remember that man, being a microcosm, comprehends and represents the whole and each.
(26) The aim in the Work is to reverse the whole action of these lives in their natural order. Man is
fallen; he is in the third or lowest life, and this third life has to be raised up and born through the
medial or celestial life, and becomes receptive of the Divine. Man must not dare to aspire to that
highest life which is over this birth and which is typified by the terrestrial or head life, since if he were
there he would become Creative --- he would be taking to himself the Divine Will.
(27) That which in the hands of fools is nothing in that of a philosopher becomes to be all in all ---
says one; all depends on the willing and the intention --- ex fine cujusvis intentionis resultat
principium ejus, and so, too, it is true and less paradoxical that finis ab origine pendet. The difficulty is
to find this principium, the intention; for the rational ferment, the pure aurific seed of Light, is hidden
in this life of ours.
