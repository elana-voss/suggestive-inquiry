## E0323 | term | conjunction
forms: conjunction / Conjunction
variants listed by readers (may include other names): Conjonction / conjunctions
- [ATWOOD] conjunction: The union (of Mars and Venus, ¶11) during which a spirituous vapour rises; in ¶13 its 'various hours' bring Darkness, Saturn, the Head of the Crow. | “in the various hours of conjunction” @III.3 ¶13
- [ATWOOD] Conjunction: Union of the principles: a first conjunction (Spirit predominant) and a second (Soul's Light predominant); the final Conjunction consummates the mysteries of the Microcosm, generates an offspring unlike/more perfect than its parents, and cannot be dissolved. | “In the first conjunction the Spirit predominates; in the second the Soul, i.e., its Light” @III.3 ¶30; “that Conjunction will generate a branch of unlike shape to its parents” @III.3 ¶29 | = true circulated Form of Gold (“This then is the Conjunction in which all the mysteries of the Microcosm have their consummation --- the true circulated Form of Gold” @III.3 ¶33); = tetraptive (Ripley); fountain of Pythagoras; Divine Tetractys (“the Conjunction, by Ripley called tetraptive, that so highly commended fountain of Pythagoras, and Divine Tetractys” @III.3 ¶33)
- [ATWOOD] Conjunction: That in which the agent further multiplies (Atwood); Norton separately speaks of 'God's conjunctions' which man may not undo without grace. | “further multiplying in the Conjunction” @III.4 ¶9; “For God’s conjunctions man my not undoe” @III.4 ¶10
- [ATWOOD] conjunction: A period in which the Spirit transcends earthly control and recreates under the Divine Hand alone. | “There is a period too, when in conjunction, the Spirit transcends all earthly control” @III.4 ¶20

## E0324 | term | connate spirit
