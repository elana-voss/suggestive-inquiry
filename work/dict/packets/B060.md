## E1815 | term | Typhon
forms: Typhon
- [ATWOOD] Typhon: In the fable, Osiris's uterine brother, who killed and scattered him. Applied to the work, Typhon is the wrathful fire or Efficient of the dissolution, and also a name of the unclean Instrument that moves the gold to putrefy. He is called Seth and Bebo, names that import violent restraint, contrariety and subversion. Isis gradually enthrals him. | “importing a certain violent, forcible restraint, contrariety, and subversion” @III.1 ¶63 | = Green Lion, Firedrake (“Adepts call him the Green Lion, Typhon, Firedrake” @III.1 ¶56); = Black Toad (during the mortification) (“or during the mortification, he is their venomous Black Toad” @III.1 ¶56); = the Instrument moving the gold to putrefy; Satan, Aquafoetida, Ignis Gehanna, Mortis Immundities (“it is called by them Typhon, Satan, Aquafoetida, Ignis Gehanna, Mortis Immundities, etc.” @III.1 ¶61); = Seth, Bebo (“they gave to Typhon, in this predicament, the name of Seth, Bebo” @III.1 ¶63)
