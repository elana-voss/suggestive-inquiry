# II.3_04

*Atwood presents the culminating vision of the Mysteries as an Introspection of formless Divine Light within the soul, rejects astronomical 'orrery' explanations, gathers Platonist, Indian, scriptural and Hermetic testimony to an inner light kindled in the soul, and identifies the Hermetic Stone, Salt and Prester with that Light or Power hidden in man, whose multiplication and placing in the supreme Ether is the perfection of the Art.*

## Argument

- Only the identical experience attains it; reason grasps it only as inference, yet it is her true Hypostasis, sought as Isis seeks Osiris. Only reason seeing more in causation than antecedence reaches her Law, where the true Efficient lies, not externally developed. `II.3 ¶46` — ATWOOD
- Conjoined in consciousness, the soul knows herself as a Whole; by theurgic assistance she participates of Deity, perceives the supplier of life present (Plotinus), and believes her Law's revelation, which is herself. `II.3 ¶46` — ATWOOD
- This is Psellus's Introspection, distinct from the Superinspection in Hades: the initiate sees the formless Divine Light, the oracle's Sacro Sancto; it will not deceive. Oracle: seeing a Fire without Form, hear the voice of Fire. `II.3 ¶46, II.3 ¶47` — QUOTED: Psellus; the Oracle (endorsed)
- Parallels: an Indian record (tr. Sir William Jones) calls all mental appearances except the First Cause maya; Deuteronomy and the Zohar: Divinity is formless, not to be imaged. `II.3 ¶48` — QUOTED: Indian record; Deuteronomy; Zohar (endorsed)
- Explaining these visions as orreries (unfigured lights asteroids, figured ones constellations) is modern folly that trivializes allegories, symbols and rites as mere Astronomy. `II.3 ¶49` — ATWOOD
- Proclus: as initiates feel astonishment and dread before the mystic spectacles, Beauty astonishes and converts the soul before participation of the Good. Apuleius: knowledge gained separated from body flashes like light in darkness. Plato: long converse and a conforming life suddenly kindle a self-nourishing inner light; heaven is the kindled intelligence of the First Intelligible. `II.3 ¶50` — QUOTED: Proclus; Apuleius; Plato (endorsed)
- Sight leads to Wisdom: its light, directed within and purified, finds the light that is its own substance, until light meeting light apprehends itself. Porphyry's verse: a sacred light seen rapt from sense, waking. `II.3 ¶50, II.3 ¶51, II.3 n57` — ATWOOD; QUOTED: Porphyry (verse, per n57)
- An adept: open the compound creature, divide the elements, find the quintessence, then the angelical spirit; the first separation is sensible, the other intellectual. `II.3 ¶52` — QUOTED: the adept
- Hermes: a furnace with our glass makes a new world, glossed as new hypostasis and Stone. Vaughan: unite heaven to earth in triple proportion with generative heat to attract the star-fore of nature. `II.3 ¶52` — QUOTED: Hermes; Vaughan (endorsed, with ATWOOD gloss)
- This is the Astrum Solis, internal spiritual Sun, Saturnian Salt; the Demogorgon, once visible without its light, now ignited. Atwood equates it with Apuleius's midnight Sun, Anaxagoras's ignited Stone, the Chariot of Antimony, Helvetius's Armed Magnet, Ezekiel's Wheel of Fire, Revelation's new-named Stone, the Salt within, Zoroaster's Prester, and John's Light. `II.3 ¶52, II.3 ¶53, II.3 ¶54` — ATWOOD
- Theologians did not invent the Light shining in darkness but declared what they had seen. It is the Power hidden in man, known only to the wisely simple who subdued their will to the Law of Wisdom, as Abraham did. `II.3 ¶55` — ATWOOD
- Mystical death and regeneration are deferred. The Art's perfection: multiply the Prester and place him in the supreme Ether, calm, no longer a Consuming Fire. `II.3 ¶56, II.3 n62` — ATWOOD

## Method

- **principle** — “a conception that transcends it, and which can only be attained through the identical experience” `II.3 ¶46` (ATWOOD)
  - **Plain (stated):** The goal cannot be grasped by ordinary intellect, explanation or analogy; it is known only by undergoing the experience itself. Reason can reach it only abstractly, as an inference.
  - Also: “Yet reason may, does perceive it, but abstractly only as an inference” `II.3 ¶46`
- **agent** — “For there the true Efficient is to be found, which is not externally developed” `II.3 ¶46` (ATWOOD)
  - **Plain (stated):** The real operative cause is found inward, in the intelligible substance of reason's own Law, not in anything external; only a reason that sees more in causation than mere antecedence can find it.
  - Also: “seeing something more in causation than mere antecedence, can reflect into the intelligible substance of her Law” `II.3 ¶46`
- **result** — “the soul knows herself as a Whole which before knew but a part only of her human nature” `II.3 ¶46` (ATWOOD)
  - **Plain (stated):** When united in consciousness, the soul comes to know her whole nature instead of only a part; she participates of Deity, perceives the source of life present, and recognizes the revelation of her Law as her own self.
  - Also: “believes its revelation which is her very self” `II.3 ¶46`
  - Also: “that the supplier of life is present” `II.3 ¶46`
- **procedure** — “by theurgic assistance, arrives at her desired end” `II.3 ¶46` (ATWOOD)
  - **Plain (stated):** The soul reaches her end with 'theurgic assistance'; this chunk does not explain what that assistance consists of.
- **stage** — “This is the Introspection which Psellus speaks of, as distinguished from the Superinspection which takes place in Hades” `II.3 ¶46` (QUOTED: Psellus (endorsed))
  - **Plain (stated):** An inward seeing (Introspection) of the Divine Light, distinguished from a 'Superinspection' located in Hades; the chunk says no more about Superinspection.
- **sign** — “the initiated person sees the Divine Light itself without any form or figure” `II.3 ¶46` (QUOTED: Psellus; the Oracle (endorsed))
  - **Plain (stated):** The true sign of the highest stage is a light seen without any shape or image; it 'will not deceive', and the instruction on seeing it is to attend to its 'voice'.
  - Also: “When thou seest a Fire without Form, Shining flashingly through the depths of the World, Hear the voice of Fire” `II.3 ¶47`
  - Also: “This will not deceive” `II.3 ¶46`
  - Also: “the unfigured form of the Divine Essence” `II.3 ¶48`
- **impediment** — “know that to be the mind’s maya (or image or delusion) as of light or darkness” `II.3 ¶48` (QUOTED: Indian record translated by Sir William Jones (endorsed as 'the same solemn and articulate instruction'))
  - **Plain (stated):** Anything that appears in the mind other than the First Cause, whether as light or darkness, is to be treated as the mind's own image or delusion, not the goal.
  - Also: “it was forbidden to represent Him under any image whatever, even so much as a letter or point” `II.3 ¶48`
- **sign** — “those who are initiated are said to be seized with astonishment and dread” `II.3 ¶50` (QUOTED: Proclus (endorsed))
  - **Plain (stated):** Before the vision proper, the initiate experiences astonishment and dread; correspondingly Beauty, shining forth before participation of the Good, astonishes the beholder and turns the soul toward itself.
  - Also: “prior to the participation of the Good, Beauty shining forth astonishes those that behold it, converts the soul to itself” `II.3 ¶50`
- **sign** — “this knowledge sometimes shines forth with a most rapid coruscation like a bright and clear light in the most profound darkness” `II.3 ¶50` (QUOTED: Apuleius (endorsed))
  - **Plain (stated):** The intellectual contact, gained when the wise were separated from body through the energies of mind, appears as a sudden flash of clear light in deep darkness.
  - Also: “when they were separated from body, through the energies of mind” `II.3 ¶50`
- **procedure** — “from long converse with this thing itself, accompanied by a life in conformity to it, on a sudden a light, as it were a leaping fire, will be enkindled in the soul” `II.3 ¶50` (QUOTED: Plato, seventh Epistle (endorsed))
  - **Plain (stated):** Prolonged engagement with the object, together with a way of life conformed to it, leads to a sudden inner light that then sustains itself.
  - Also: “will there itself nourish itself” `II.3 ¶50`
- **procedure** — “directed within, and being purified also, and scientifically inquiring, discovers at last that other light which is the substance of its own, until light meeting light apprehends itself alone” `II.3 ¶50` (ATWOOD)
  - **Plain (stated):** The faculty of seeing is turned inward, purified and used in methodical inquiry, until it finds the light that is its own substance and knows itself alone; this is why sight is called a leader to Wisdom.
  - Also: “as a leader in the acquisition of Wisdom” `II.3 ¶50`
- **sign** — “Nor was the vision like the dreams of sleep, But seen whilst vigilant you brave the deep” `II.3 ¶51` (QUOTED: Porphyry's Hymn to Plotinus (per n57))
  - **Plain (stated):** The vision of the sacred light occurs in a waking state, not in dream, while the subject is 'rapt from sense'.
  - Also: “Whence rapt from sense with energy divine” `II.3 ¶51`
- **procedure** — “Open the compound creature; look up the elements; divide the elements, and you shall find the quintessential nature” `II.3 ¶52` (QUOTED: the adept (unnamed))
  - **Plain (stated):** The work proceeds by analysis: open the compound, separate its elements, find the quintessence; opening that reveals the 'angelical spirit', the divine act and beam of Wisdom from God. The chunk does not say what the 'compound creature' is.
  - Also: “open this, continues the adept, and you shall conceive the subtle altereity of the angelical spirit in which is the divine act, and immediate beam or Wisdom from God” `II.3 ¶52`
- **stage** — “there concurreth in the separation of the first, a sensible aspect, in the other we behold with intellectual eyes” `II.3 ¶52` (UNCLEAR)
  - **Plain (stated):** Two levels of separation: the first is perceived by the senses, the second only by intellectual sight.
- **procedure** — “He that maketh a furnace with our glass to it maketh a new world” `II.3 ¶52` (QUOTED: Hermes, with ATWOOD gloss)
  - **Plain (stated):** Setting up the furnace with 'our glass' produces a 'new world', which Atwood glosses as a new hypostasis and a new stone. Furnace and glass are not further explained here.
  - Basis: Atwood's gloss follows directly: 'a new hypostasis, and a new stone' (II.3 ¶52).
  - Also: “a new hypostasis, and a new stone” `II.3 ¶52`
- **result** — “that renowned Terra Maga in aethere clarificata, which carries in its belly wind and fire” `II.3 ¶52` (ATWOOD)
  - **Plain (stated):** The new stone is described as a spotless, clear crystalline rock containing wind and fire, identified with the Stone of the Apocalypse; 'Terra Maga in aethere clarificata' is untranslated in the book (our rendering: magical earth clarified in the ether).
  - Also: “even that Stone of the Apocalypse, the true crystalline rock without spot or darkness” `II.3 ¶52`
- **procedure** — “unite the heaven in triple proportion to the earth, and then apply a generative heat to both” `II.3 ¶52` (QUOTED: Vaughan (endorsed))
  - **Plain (stated):** Having the 'little new world', unite 'heaven' to 'earth' in 'triple proportion' and apply generative heat, so that they draw down from above the 'star-fore of nature'. The chunk does not explain heaven, earth, the proportion or the heat.
  - Also: “they will attract from above the star-fore of nature” `II.3 ¶52`
- **result** — “This is the true Astrum Solis gotten and conceived, the internal spiritual Sun which is the Perpetual Motion of the Wise” `II.3 ¶52` (ATWOOD)
  - **Plain (stated):** What is obtained is an inner spiritual sun; Atwood also calls it the Saturnian Salt which, developed to intellect, subdues all nature to its will.
  - Also: “that Saturnian Salt which, developed to intellect and made erect, subdues all nature to his will” `II.3 ¶52`
- **stage** — “the whole Demogorgon, now actually animated, which before was made visible without its subject light; but at length becoming ignited” `II.3 ¶52` (ATWOOD)
  - **Plain (interpreted):** A prior state in which the subject was made visible but without its own light is followed by ignition, when it becomes animated and shines out of the dark abyss of being like a radiant wheel.
  - Basis: II.3 ¶52: 'before was made visible without its subject light' contrasted with 'at length becoming ignited, reflects from out the dark abyss of being, as a luciferous wheel'; the identity of the Demogorgon is not stated.
  - Also: “reflects from out the dark abyss of being, as a luciferous wheel, with its radiant sections” `II.3 ¶52`
- **result** — “And this we take to be that midnight Sun of Apuleius, the ignited Stone of Anaxagoras” `II.3 ¶54` (ATWOOD)
  - **Plain (stated):** Atwood identifies the ignited inner light/Stone with a series of traditional images: Apuleius's midnight Sun, Anaxagoras's ignited Stone (wrongly taken as the visible sun), the Chariot of Antimony, Helvetius's Armed Magnet, Ezekiel's Wheel of Fire, and the Stone with the new name in Revelation.
  - Also: “This is the triumphal Chariot of Antimony, the Armed Magnet of Helvetius turned swiftly about the current axle of life, which is the Wheel of Fire signalized in Ezekiel” `II.3 ¶54`
  - Also: “this is the Stone with the new name written in the Revelation” `II.3 ¶54`
- **agent** — “the Prester of Zoroaster which in the Chaldean sense means the Fire Spirit of Life” `II.3 ¶54` (ATWOOD)
  - **Plain (stated):** The Prester is the fire-spirit of life: the one identity in all things that sustains them, the supernatural centre of every living thing and the all-efficient making power.
  - Also: “that Identity in all which sustains all by the efflux of His power --- the supernatural center of every living thing, the infinitely powerful and all-efficient making power” `II.3 ¶54`
- **subject** — “This therefore is that Power which is hidden in man, the true Light which lighteth every man that cometh into the world” `II.3 ¶55` (ATWOOD)
  - **Plain (stated):** The Stone/Salt/Light is a power hidden within the human being; the Salt is something we are told to have 'in ourselves'.
  - Also: “that Salt which the Savior orders that we should have it in ourselves” `II.3 ¶54`
- **operator** — “known only to the wisely simple who have subdued their will to the Law of Wisdom” `II.3 ¶55` (ATWOOD)
  - **Plain (stated):** The operator must be 'wisely simple' and have submitted his own will to the Law of Wisdom; Abraham is the example, 'as soon as he had gotten the creature into his hands' (not explained here).
  - Also: “as Abraham did, as soon as he had gotten the creature into his hands” `II.3 ¶55`
- **result** — “the grand perfection of their Art was to multiply the Prester and place him in the most supreme Ether” `II.3 ¶56` (ATWOOD (reporting 'our doctors'))
  - **Plain (stated):** The final aim is to increase the life-fire and establish it in the highest Ether, its proper prepared dwelling, where it no longer burns destructively but shines calm, vital and transmuting.
  - Also: “he abides shining, not burning as below, or wrathful; but vital, calm, transmuting, recreating, and no longer a Consuming Fire” `II.3 ¶56`
- **procedure** — “et statue rem integritate et fac sedere Creatorm in throno suo” `II.3 ¶56` (QUOTED: Sepher Jezirah / Liber de Creatione, Authore Abraham)
  - **Plain (interpreted):** On our translation only: understand, test, investigate, note, think and imagine, establish the thing in its integrity, and make the Creator sit on his throne. The book gives no translation; no claim rests on this alone.
  - Basis: Based solely on our translation of the Latin at II.3 ¶56 (source per II.3 n62).
  - Also: “Intellige in scientia et sepias intellignetia: exprerire in illis, et investiga illa, et nota, et cogita, et imaginare” `II.3 ¶56`

## Terms

- **Introspection** — The initiate's inward seeing of the Divine Light without form or figure, as spoken of by Psellus; distinguished from the Superinspection. (QUOTED: Psellus)
  - “This is the Introspection which Psellus speaks of, as distinguished from the Superinspection which takes place in Hades” `II.3 ¶46`
- **Superinspection** — A seeing that takes place in Hades, distinguished from the Introspection; not further explained here. (QUOTED: Psellus)
  - “the Superinspection which takes place in Hades” `II.3 ¶46`
- **Hypostasis** (also: hypostasis) — Reason's true Hypostasis is her objective reality in the Great Unknown, which she constantly seeks; the 'new world' made with furnace and glass is glossed as a new hypostasis. (ATWOOD)
  - = new world / new stone: “maketh a new world (58); --- a new hypostasis, and a new stone” `II.3 ¶52`
  - “it is her true Hypostasis, for which, as Isis for Osiris, she is constantly seeking, her objective reality in the Great Unknown” `II.3 ¶46`
- **Efficient** — The true efficient cause, found in the intelligible substance of reason's Law and not externally developed. (ATWOOD)
  - “For there the true Efficient is to be found, which is not externally developed” `II.3 ¶46`
- **Law** (also: Law of Wisdom) — The intelligible law of reason/soul, whose 'circular necessity' includes the perfected soul and whose revelation is the soul's very self; also the Law of Wisdom to which the wisely simple subdue their will. (ATWOOD)
  - “percipiently included in the circular necessity of her Law, believes its revelation which is her very self” `II.3 ¶46`
  - “who have subdued their will to the Law of Wisdom” `II.3 ¶55`
- **Divine Light** (also: Fire without Form) — The light without form or figure seen by the initiate in the Introspection; it will not deceive. (QUOTED: Psellus; the Oracle)
  - = Sacro Sancto: “this the oracle calls Sacro Sancto” `II.3 ¶46`
  - “the initiated person sees the Divine Light itself without any form or figure” `II.3 ¶46`
  - “When thou seest a Fire without Form” `II.3 ¶47`
- **maya** — Whatever appears or does not appear in the mind except the First Cause: the mind's image or delusion. (QUOTED: Indian record)
  - “know that to be the mind’s maya (or image or delusion) as of light or darkness” `II.3 ¶48`
- **Intellectual Intuition** — Plato's term as given here for the sudden kindling of a self-nourishing light in the soul after long converse and conforming life. (QUOTED: Plato)
  - “speaking in like manner of the Intellectual Intuition” `II.3 ¶50`
- **quintessential nature** — What is found when the elements of the compound creature are divided; opened further, it yields the angelical spirit. (QUOTED: the adept)
  - “divide the elements, and you shall find the quintessential nature” `II.3 ¶52`
- **angelical spirit** — What is conceived on opening the quintessential nature; it contains the divine act and is the immediate beam of Wisdom from God. (QUOTED: the adept)
  - “the subtle altereity of the angelical spirit in which is the divine act, and immediate beam or Wisdom from God” `II.3 ¶52`
- **Stone** (also: new stone, Stone of the Apocalypse, ignited Stone) — The product of the furnace with 'our glass'; a spotless crystalline rock carrying wind and fire; identified with the internal Light/Power. (ATWOOD)
  - = new hypostasis: “a new hypostasis, and a new stone” `II.3 ¶52`
  - = Terra Maga in aethere clarificata: “even that Stone of the Apocalypse, the true crystalline rock without spot or darkness, that renowned Terra Maga in aethere clarificata” `II.3 ¶52`
  - = Stone with the new name in Revelation: “this is the Stone with the new name written in the Revelation” `II.3 ¶54`
  - “which carries in its belly wind and fire” `II.3 ¶52`
- **Astrum Solis** — The internal spiritual Sun, gotten and conceived through the work; identified with a chain of traditional images. (ATWOOD)
  - = internal spiritual Sun; Perpetual Motion of the Wise; Saturnian Salt: “This is the true Astrum Solis gotten and conceived, the internal spiritual Sun which is the Perpetual Motion of the Wise, and that Saturnian Salt” `II.3 ¶52`
  - = midnight Sun of Apuleius; ignited Stone of Anaxagoras: “And this we take to be that midnight Sun of Apuleius, the ignited Stone of Anaxagoras” `II.3 ¶54`
  - = Chariot of Antimony; Armed Magnet of Helvetius; Wheel of Fire: “This is the triumphal Chariot of Antimony, the Armed Magnet of Helvetius turned swiftly about the current axle of life, which is the Wheel of Fire signalized in Ezekiel” `II.3 ¶54`
- **Salt** (also: Saturnian Salt) — The Saturnian Salt which, developed to intellect and made erect, subdues all nature; also the Salt the Savior orders us to have in ourselves. (ATWOOD)
  - = Astrum Solis: “the internal spiritual Sun which is the Perpetual Motion of the Wise, and that Saturnian Salt” `II.3 ¶52`
  - “that Salt which the Savior orders that we should have it in ourselves” `II.3 ¶54`
- **Demogorgon** — Something formerly made visible without its subject light, which becomes animated and ignited and shines from the dark abyss of being as a luciferous wheel; not otherwise defined here. (ATWOOD)
  - “For it is the whole Demogorgon, now actually animated, which before was made visible without its subject light” `II.3 ¶52`
- **Prester** — Zoroaster's term, in the Chaldean sense the Fire Spirit of Life: the identity sustaining all, the supernatural centre of every living thing; the Art's perfection was to multiply it and place it in the supreme Ether. (ATWOOD)
  - = Fire Spirit of Life: “the Prester of Zoroaster which in the Chaldean sense means the Fire Spirit of Life” `II.3 ¶54`
  - “to multiply the Prester and place him in the most supreme Ether” `II.3 ¶56`
- **Ether** (also: most supreme Ether) — The highest region where the multiplied Prester is placed, its habitation prepared from the beginning. (ATWOOD)
  - = Augean palace: “the most supreme Ether, which is that Augean palace already prepared for him in the beginning” `II.3 ¶56`
- **Consuming Fire** — The burning, wrathful state of the Prester 'below', which it ceases to be when placed in the supreme Ether. (ATWOOD)
  - “not burning as below, or wrathful; but vital, calm, transmuting, recreating, and no longer a Consuming Fire” `II.3 ¶56`
- **Power hidden in man** — The Light shining in darkness, present within man; an enchanted treasury known only to the wisely simple. (ATWOOD)
  - = the true Light: “This therefore is that Power which is hidden in man, the true Light which lighteth every man that cometh into the world” `II.3 ¶55`
  - “An enchanted treasury known only to the wisely simple” `II.3 ¶55`
- **Greater Mysteries** — The rites whose visions and awful accompaniments the gathered revelations partly describe. (ATWOOD)
  - “the visions, and awful accompaniments which took place in the celebration of the Greater Mysteries” `II.3 ¶49`
- **mystical death and regeneration** — A topic omitted here, to be better understood with the manifestation of the Philosophic Subject. (ATWOOD)
  - “We omit many things here relating to the mystical death and regeneration” `II.3 ¶56`
- **Philosophic Subject** — Named only as a later topic whose manifestation will clarify the mystical death and regeneration. (ATWOOD)
  - “when we come to treat of the manifestation of the Philosophic Subject” `II.3 ¶56`

## Ambiguities

- “Open the compound creature” `II.3 ¶52`
  - Reading: The human being/soul as the subject to be opened and divided
  - Reading: A compound subject worked on with furnace and glass
  - Basis: Reading 1: the Power is 'hidden in man' (¶55), the Salt is to be had 'in ourselves' (¶54), Abraham 'had gotten the creature into his hands' (¶55), and the soul comes to know herself as a Whole (¶46). Reading 2: 'divide the elements' and 'He that maketh a furnace with our glass' (¶52) use operative, vessel language.
- “the whole Demogorgon, now actually animated, which before was made visible without its subject light” `II.3 ¶52`
  - Reading: The soul/being as subject, first manifested without its light, then ignited
  - Reading: The worked matter of the 'little new world', first visible, then ignited
  - Basis: Reading 1: 'reflects from out the dark abyss of being' (¶52) and the Power 'hidden in man' (¶55). Reading 2: follows directly on Vaughan's 'little new world' with applied 'generative heat' (¶52).
- “unite the heaven in triple proportion to the earth” `II.3 ¶52`
  - Reading: A literal proportion of two components in the vessel
  - Reading: A figurative proportion of higher to lower parts of the 'little new world'
  - Basis: The chunk gives no gloss; only the context of 'this fundamental of a little new world' and 'generative heat' (¶52).
- “the star-fore of nature” `II.3 ¶52`
  - Reading: A term as printed whose meaning the chunk does not give
  - Reading: A misprint; the chunk offers no correction
  - Basis: Occurs once, unexplained, in the Vaughan quotation (¶52).
- “that beautiful sun-lit horizon of the Maudeurentian, rising to intellectual radiance” `II.3 ¶50`
  - Reading: A name for the higher (lighted) stage or region of the Mysteries' vision
  - Reading: A corrupt or obscure word whose referent the chunk does not give
  - Basis: Contrasted with 'the darker scenes of life’s drama' and followed by Proclus on Beauty shining forth (¶50); the word is not otherwise explained.

## Threads

- `II.3 ¶49` 'the revelations we have here gathered' refers back to visions of the Greater Mysteries described before this chunk.
- `II.3 ¶50` 'we continue by our clue, leaving the darker scenes of life’s drama' points back to the darker scenes treated earlier.
- `II.3 ¶52` 'as the Oracle again proclaims' points back to the Oracle cited at ¶47 (and possibly earlier).
- `II.3 ¶54` 'that midnight Sun of Apuleius' presupposes an Apuleius passage on a midnight Sun not given in this chunk.
- `II.3 ¶56` Mystical death and regeneration deferred to the later treatment of 'the manifestation of the Philosophic Subject'.
