Suggestive Inquiry: Summaries

The Conclusion

Part IV, Chapter 4

The Conclusion sums up the Inquiry, answers objections, and ends with directions for the qualified seeker. Its main claim: the Hermetic work is done in man on a universal principle within him, and “Life is the nucleus of the whole Hermetic Mystery, and the Key thereof is Light” (IV.4 ¶2–4, ¶32).

The claims restated

Atwood says she has only “signalized the Light” (IV.4 ¶2). Transmutation concerns not Species but “their Universal Subject”, whose concentrated virtue the Stone is, traced from “the free Ether” through artificial elaboration into effect (IV.4 ¶2). “Man was the proper laboratory of the whole Art”, holding every requisite within, his power hidden by “external attraction” (IV.4 ¶3). Reporting the adepts with approval, she says man’s life is “a pure fire” in an ethereal vapour holding the Universal Efficient (IV.4 ¶4). The Mysteries were “real inductions of the Understanding Spirit to its Source”, and Mesmerism is “a first key” to their vestibule (IV.4 ¶5). The “ray of motive Light” is “the true Form of Gold”; dominant in the circulation, it becomes concrete in life as the Stone (IV.4 ¶9). The practical device passed almost only by word of mouth (IV.4 ¶7).

Why Hermetic Science is the best philosophy

Physical science rests on sense, moral science on mind; neither can disprove the other, and Hermetic Science would include both, reaching “the Catholic Effect of Life” (IV.4 ¶11). Knowledge without experience is “empirical”; Nature’s working is unknown “without Identic co-operation”, a term the text leaves undefined (IV.4 ¶12). True science is “Causal Discovery” (IV.4 ¶13).

Answering objections

The doctrine has “a strongly enchanted fortress” against curiosity and sense (IV.4 ¶14). Outward improvement cannot reach inbred evil: “That which generation binds dissolution only can unloose”, by Light overcoming darkness (IV.4 ¶17–18). Reason’s Source lies buried in sense-life; the Art resuscitates it “through self-knowledge”, and “this same Root of Reason is Wisdom, and that saving Salt” (IV.4 ¶20). The regenerate soul puts on the Stone “as a new body”; no faculty is rejected, all being renewed with “their King” (IV.4 ¶20) — whether the King is Reason or its Source the text leaves open. Answering Job, she says success comes “by conditionating, in obedience to the Divine Will” (IV.4 ¶23); the Art is “a remedy of imperfect souls” (IV.4 ¶25).

The Nothing and the Key

The Wisdom comes only “by the searching and proving of His own Identity” (IV.4 ¶27). It is “that Nothing which Socrates knew”: he who knows “no one apostate particular” has the All; becoming “dividual” he loses it, and by abandoning selfhood regains it (IV.4 ¶27). Modern science, sense-bound and selfish, has lost it (IV.4 ¶28–30). The seeker should “search for the Root of Reason rationally”; whoever knows “the first entrance, and how to render the fixed tincture of life volatile, and to return it” enters “the temple of Divine Science” (IV.4 ¶32).

The Work in this chapter

  • Matter: “a Universal Subject … susceptible of nourishment in Man” (IV.4 ¶10) — one principle, fed within the person (stated).
  • Vessel: Man as “laboratory” (IV.4 ¶3) — the work is inward (stated).
  • Agent: “ray of motive Light” (IV.4 ¶9) — living light, the Form of Gold and the Stone (stated).
  • Operator: “a true lover of Wisdom” (IV.4 ¶8), obedient to the Divine Will (IV.4 ¶23) (stated).
  • Impediment: “self-idolatry” and becoming “dividual” (IV.4 ¶32, ¶27) — selfhood (interpreted).
  • Operation: “render the fixed tincture of life volatile, and to return it” (IV.4 ¶32) — free the life-principle and return it; unexplained (interpreted).
  • Result: “a new birth, superior to nature” (IV.4 ¶12); co-operation “with the First Cause” (IV.4 ¶32) (stated).

Terms introduced

  • Universal Subject — the one principle underlying all kinds, nourishable in man (IV.4 ¶2, ¶10).
  • Form of Gold — the ray of motive Light, the Stone (IV.4 ¶9).
  • pyrotechnical induction — the vital experiment (IV.4 ¶9).
  • Catholic Effect of Life — where inner and outer worlds blend (IV.4 ¶11).
  • Identic co-operation — undefined condition for knowing Nature (IV.4 ¶12).
  • Causal Discovery — knowledge of causes, true science (IV.4 ¶13).
  • Root of Reason — Reason’s buried Source; Wisdom and the saving Salt (IV.4 ¶20).
  • conditionating — submitting to conditions under the Divine Will (IV.4 ¶23).
  • Nothing — holding no separate particular (IV.4 ¶27).
  • dividual — separated selfhood (IV.4 ¶27).
  • Key — Light, which opens the Mystery (IV.4 ¶32).
  • fixed tincture of life — to be volatilized and returned (IV.4 ¶32).

Authorities cited

Adepts (life as fire, IV.4 ¶4; God’s revelation to the elect, ¶25); Greek Ontologists (¶6); Alchemists and Greek physicians (immutable Being, ¶13); Job (answered) and St Paul (¶23–24); Heraclitus, Socrates, the oracle, Democritus, Friar Bacon, Agrippa (the Nothing, ¶27); Platonists, Kabbalah, Hermetic and Chaldaic Fragments, Adeptists, the Gospel (one tradition, ¶28); Lord bacon (criticized yet urging Causes, ¶30).
